How It Works

A closer look at the new equipment and app going into Botanic @ Cluny Park, and how each part works day to day.

Akuvox S535 Visitor Call Panel

2 units · Main entrance and exit gate

The Akuvox S535 is a smart audio-video visitor call panel with an aluminium panel and a 5-inch touchscreen. It supports face recognition, RFID cards, PINs, Bluetooth and QR codes, so entry is quick for residents and simple for visitors. Its wide-angle camera gives a clear view of the person at the door, and it is built for outdoor use.

  • Residents enter with facial recognition or the SmartPlus mobile app
  • Visitors call residents directly from the panel
  • Installed at the main entrance and the exit gate (2 units)
  • It replaces the existing intercom

Hikvision CCTV Cameras

2 cameras · Entrance and exit

Two Hikvision 6MP cameras with a 180° wide view are installed on poles at the gate. They record to a new 4-channel recorder.

  • Entrance camera: looks outside the gate
  • Exit camera: looks at vehicles as they leave the condominium

SmartPlus Mobile App

Your phone as your key

SmartPlus is the mobile app that links your phone to the new intercom system. Once your account is activated, you can receive visitor calls, view live video and open the gate remotely, wherever you are. After the upgrade, the existing monitor in your house will not be used to open the gate, so please use the app.

  • Remote gate opening, live video and notifications
  • Door logs, and a temporary key (QR code) for visitors
  • Register your email by 4 December 2026 so your account can be set up
  • Accounts are activated on 9 December 2026, after which you can download the app
  • Each household may register up to 4 mobile app users

Ready to register?

Your details are needed to activate your SmartPlus account. Please register by 4 December 2026.
