How It Works

A closer look at the equipment proposed for The Mercury, and how each part would work day to day.

Akuvox X915 Visitor Call Panel

2 units · Side gate and Level 1 passenger lift lobby

The Akuvox X915 is a smart audio-video visitor call panel with an 8-inch IPS screen and dual 2MP cameras with starlight. Residents can enter with face recognition, card, PIN, the mobile app or a QR code, and visitors call the resident directly from the panel. It is IP65 rated and IK10 vandal proof, and is mounted on a stainless steel plate with an instruction plate.

  • Residents enter by face, card, PIN, app or QR code
  • Visitors call residents from the panel and the call rings on the SmartPlus app
  • Installed at the side gate and the Level 1 passenger lift lobby (2 units)
  • Existing intercom cabling is reused where possible

Akuvox S535 Visitor Call Panel

1 unit · Carpark entrance

The Akuvox S535 is a smart audio-video visitor call panel with a 5-inch touchscreen and a wide-angle camera. It supports face recognition, EM/Mifare cards, PINs, Bluetooth and QR codes, with two-way audio and echo cancellation. It is IP66 and IK08 rated, comes with a rain shield and is mounted on a stainless steel plate with an instruction plate.

  • Residents enter by face, card, PIN, Bluetooth, app or QR code
  • Visitors call residents directly from the panel
  • Installed at the carpark entrance

Akuvox A02S IP Access Reader

1 unit · Level 2 gym room

The Akuvox A02S is an IP access reader with a touch keypad. It supports PIN, RFID cards and mobile access, holds up to 20,000 cards and is IP65 rated. It controls entry to the Level 2 gym room, and the existing door accessories are reused.

  • Residents enter with PIN, RFID card or mobile access
  • Installed at the Level 2 gym room

Lift Controllers and Guard House

2 lift controllers · 1 guard portal

Two Akuvox EC33 elevator controllers link the lifts to the access system, and an Akuvox VP-R49G Android video phone gives the guard house a portal to the system.

  • Lift controllers are installed with the lift vendor present
  • Guard house video phone has a 7-inch touchscreen and 2MP camera
  • How lift floors are controlled for residents and visitors is to be confirmed with the council and the lift vendor

Hikvision CCTV System

17 cameras · 32-channel recorder

A new Hikvision 32-channel recorder with an 8TB disk stores up to one month of 24/7 recording at 1080p and 12 frames per second. The new cameras cover the main entrance guardhouse, lift lobbies, letterbox area, carpark driveways and exits, the basement ramp and carpark, the gym room and the swimming pool.

  • 2MP ColorVu cameras give colour images at night
  • 6MP 180° cameras cover the carpark driveway, carpark area and swimming pool
  • Water and dust resistant (IP67) with up to 30 m infrared range
  • Exact camera positions are agreed with the council on site

Barrier Gates and Licence Plate Recognition

2 barriers · 2 LPR cameras · GantryGo

FAAC B614 barrier gates are installed at the entry and exit, each with a Milesight AI licence plate recognition camera on its own pole. The cameras read vehicle plates and the GantryGo software, installed on a desktop computer with a monitor, manages vehicle entry. GantryGo cloud hosting and servicing includes the camera licence and on-site and remote support during office hours.

  • Entry barrier with 3.5 m arm; exit barrier with 3.5 m articulating arm
  • AI licence plate recognition at both barriers
  • GantryGo software set up and hosted in the cloud
  • How residents’ and visitors’ vehicles are registered is to be confirmed with the council

SmartPlus Mobile App

Your phone as your key

SmartPlus is the mobile app that links your phone to the new system. Once your account is activated you can receive visitor calls, view live video and open doors remotely, wherever you are. Each household may have up to 4 App Users, with 69 licences in total for the 67 resident units, the MCST and the Managing Agent.

  • Remote door opening, live video and notifications
  • Door logs, and a temporary key (QR code) for visitors
  • Residents register their email once so the account can be set up
  • Registration dates are to be confirmed
