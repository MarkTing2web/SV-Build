Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the proposed upgrade. Dates are illustrative and items marked “to be confirmed” will be settled with the council.

The proposal

What is being proposed?

A full upgrade of the intercom and access control system, a new CCTV system, and barrier gates with licence plate recognition for the entry and exit. Residents would use the SmartPlus mobile app. The System Overview lists every item.

Is this confirmed?

No. This page is a preview for the council. Equipment, scope and dates remain subject to the council’s approval of Securevision’s quotation.

When would the work be done?

The dates on this page are illustrative: registration from 5 to 15 January 2027, verification on 18 January, app activation on 20 January and go-live on 21 January 2027. They will be confirmed with the council after approval, and the same dates will appear on every page, letter and email.

Residents and the SmartPlus app

What would residents need to do?

Register their email once on the registration form, so the SmartPlus account can be set up. Please register by Friday, 15 January 2027 (illustrative date). The Managing Agent would share a letter with a QR code that opens the page. Registration never closes; the date is when the details are verified.

How many people can use the app in each household?

Up to 4 App Users per household. The system has 69 licences, for the 67 resident units, the MCST and the Managing Agent.

What can I do with the SmartPlus app?

Receive visitor calls from the intercom, view live video, open doors remotely, receive notifications, check door logs and create a temporary key (QR code) for a visitor.

What do I need to use the app?

A smartphone and an internet connection, either Wi-Fi or mobile data.

Will the monitor in my unit still be used?

To be confirmed with the council. The proposal covers the common-area call panels and the app; it does not include new in-unit monitors.

Installation and equipment

Where would the new call panels go?

Akuvox X915 panels at the side gate and the Level 1 passenger lift lobby, and an Akuvox S535 panel at the carpark entrance. An Akuvox A02S access reader is proposed for the Level 2 gym room.

Will the lifts be affected?

Two Akuvox EC33 elevator controllers are proposed. The lift vendor must be present during installation, so the work would be scheduled with them.

Will existing cabling be reused?

Yes, existing intercom and barrier cabling is reused wherever possible, on the basis that it is in good working condition. New cabling is installed where needed.

What does the CCTV upgrade include?

17 new Hikvision cameras and a 32-channel recorder with an 8TB disk, storing up to one month of 24/7 recording at 1080p and 12 fps.

How would vehicles enter and leave?

Two FAAC barrier gates would be installed, each with a Milesight AI licence plate recognition camera and managed by GantryGo software. How residents’ and visitors’ vehicles are registered is to be confirmed with the council.

Support

Who looks after the system after installation?

Securevision provides a 12-month warranty. GantryGo cloud hosting and servicing includes on-site and remote support during office hours, Monday to Friday, 9 am to 6 pm.
