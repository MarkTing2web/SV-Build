Intercom, Access, CCTV & Vehicle Entry Upgrade

What Would Change?

Six upgrades that work together for a safer and more convenient The Mercury.

1. Smart Intercom with Face Recognition

New Akuvox call panels at the side gate, the carpark entrance and the Level 1 passenger lift lobby. Residents are recognised by face, card, PIN or the app, and visitors call residents through the panel.

2. Lift and Gym Access

Akuvox elevator controllers for the two lifts and an IP access reader at the Level 2 gym room, on the same access system as the intercom.

3. Guard House Portal

A new Akuvox Android video phone gives the guard house a portal to the intercom and access system.

4. New CCTV System

17 Hikvision cameras across the gate, lift lobbies, carpark, basement, gym and swimming pool, recorded on a new 32-channel recorder with up to one month of 24/7 recording.

5. Barrier Gates and Licence Plate Recognition

New FAAC barrier gates at the entry and exit, with Milesight AI licence plate recognition cameras and GantryGo software to manage vehicle entry.

6. Mobile App for Residents

Use the SmartPlus app to receive visitor calls, view live video and open doors remotely. Up to 4 App Users per household.

Proposed Project Approach

All dates will be confirmed with the council after approval.

  1. To be confirmed
    Approval and Kick-off

    The council approves the proposal and we agree the schedule, site access and contacts with the Managing Agent.

  2. To be confirmed
    Resident Registration

    Residents register their email for the SmartPlus app on a simple online form, with a letter and QR code from the Managing Agent.

  3. To be confirmed
    Cabling and Installation

    Existing cabling is reused where possible. Lift work is done with the lift vendor present.

  4. To be confirmed
    Activation and Go-Live

    SmartPlus accounts are activated, Securevision staff help residents on site, and the new system goes live.
