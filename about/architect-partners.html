Working With Architects

Security Designed to Work With Architecture, Not Against It

We work closely with Singapore's leading architectural practices to integrate security systems early in the design process — delivering protection that is discreet, coherent, and built in rather than added on.

Our Approach to Collaboration

Early Engagement, Better Outcomes

Security is most effective when it is considered at the same time as the architecture. When we are brought in early — during design development or schematic design — we can route conduits, plan equipment positions, and coordinate with M&E consultants before walls are built. The result is a system that is invisible when it should be, and present when it matters.

We have worked alongside architects on detached bungalows, semi-detached homes, and landed cluster developments across Singapore. In each case, the brief is the same: security that respects the design intent of the home.

Working Together

How We Engage With Your Practice

We are experienced in working alongside architectural teams at various stages of a project. Here is how the collaboration typically works.

Design Development

Early Engagement

When engaged during design development, we can coordinate cable routes, equipment positions, and structural penetrations before construction drawings are finalised. This avoids costly retrofits and visible conduit runs.

Construction Stage

On-Site Coordination

We work directly with the main contractor and M&E sub-contractors to ensure first-fix cabling is completed correctly. Our team attends site meetings when required and provides installation drawings for record.

Completion

Handover & Training

At practical completion we commission all systems, provide user training for residents, and hand over documentation. We remain available for post-handover support and system adjustments.
