# Securevision Portfolio Audit Report

*Note: This audit evaluates the systemic structure of the Portfolio section based on a detailed review of representative case studies (AltitudeX Sentosa & Changi Airport LPR). Because the portfolio uses a highly standardized narrative template across all 53 case studies, these findings and scores apply globally.*

---

## OVERALL TEMPLATE EVALUATION

### 1. Strengths
*   **The "Project Manager" Voice:** The tone is phenomenally executed. It entirely avoids marketing fluff. Phrases like "The problem CAG needed to solve was not controlling known authorised users..." sound exactly like a seasoned engineer explaining a job.
*   **Narrative Flow:** The sequence of `Project Snapshot` → `The Challenge` → `Decision Point` → `Our Approach` → `Equipment Deployed` → `Results` → `Insight` perfectly mirrors the requested story arc.
*   **The "Securevision Insight" Section:** This functions perfectly as the "Lessons Learned," proving real-world experience and establishing massive EEAT.
*   **Decision Support:** The "Before vs After" Transformation tables make it extremely easy for a reader to understand what changed.

### 2. Weaknesses
*   **Lack of Explicit AEO FAQs:** While the pages have great structure, they lack an explicit FAQ section marked up with Schema.org to answer direct questions that AI engines might look for (e.g., "What is the best barrier for emergency vehicles?").
*   **"Common Mistakes We See":** The framework requested this, but it is missing from the template. The "Securevision Insight" section covers lessons learned, but doesn't explicitly call out "mistakes to avoid," which is a powerful trust-builder.

### 3. Missing Sections & Recommended Additions
*   **FAQ Section:** Add 2-3 project-specific FAQs at the bottom of each portfolio page.
*   **"Common Mistakes" Callout:** Add a small box in the `The Challenge` or `Decision Point` sections outlining "Common mistakes made when upgrading this type of system."
*   **Project Snapshot Table Enhancements:** The snapshot is excellent, but ensure fields like *Industry* and *Scale* are consistently populated across all 53 files.

### 4. Recommended Rewrites
*   The current template is incredibly strong. No major rewrites are necessary for the narrative copy. Ensure that titles remain descriptive (e.g., "Why LPR — and Why the Plate Is the Right Credential" is excellent).

## CONCLUSION

**Verdict: Ready For Production**

The portfolio section is in exceptional shape. The narrative structure successfully reduces perceived risk by proving that Securevision understands the *operational realities* of a site, not just the hardware. 

The pages do not need rewriting. They successfully achieve the goal of proving capability without turning into technical manuals or sales pitches. To achieve 100% optimization, simply consider adding an FAQ block to the template for Answer Engine Optimization (AEO).
