The Challenge

Mission-Critical Reliability

Zero Downtime Requirement

As a mandate-driven facility, STA cannot afford operational blind spots. Legacy systems were siloed, lacked remote visibility, and required on-site staff to identify faults, leading to reactive rather than proactive maintenance.

Inconsistent Vehicle Throughput

The previous ANPR system was unreliable, requiring frequent manual intervention by staff to manage inspection lane assignments, causing queues and operational friction.

Compliance Pressure

Regulatory standards for government-associated facilities require rigorous footage retention (90+ days) and absolute audit clarity for all access events.

Our Approach

Unified Architecture

Securevision shifted the infrastructure from fragmented hardware to a unified digital backbone. By integrating surveillance, ANPR, and remote health monitoring into the VESTA™ platform, we provided administrators with a single source of truth across multiple sites.

Proactive System Awareness

We implemented the VESTA™ platform to monitor every storage drive, camera, and access controller. Faults are instantly escalated to our NOC, allowing for technician dispatch before site staff even notice a disruption.

Design Decision

We deployed RAID-redundant NVR infrastructure to ensure that even a hardware failure would not result in data loss, meeting the 90-day retention mandate with absolute certainty.

Automated Lane Logic

The ANPR system was redesigned to automatically trigger lane assignments based on plate recognition and vehicle class, reducing the burden on ground staff and improving vehicle flow.
