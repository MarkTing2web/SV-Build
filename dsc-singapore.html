About DSC

The Enterprise Engineof Modern Intrusion Detection

DSC (Digital Security Controls) was founded in 1979 in Toronto, Canada, and is now a flagship brand within the Johnson Controls security portfolio. The company specializes in hardwired and hybrid alarm panels that serve as the backbone for large-scale enterprise security. Their PowerSeries Neo platform represents the convergence of high-reliability hardwired panels with the PowerG wireless protocol — allowing for a unified system that covers vast physical distances. DSC is the global benchmark for commercial alarm systems, specified by more multinational corporations and government agencies than any other panel manufacturer.

DSC has been the primary commercial alarm choice in Singapore for decades, supported by a deep network of certified technical support and local inventory. Their panels are fully compliant with the UL and EN 50131 Grade 2 and Grade 3 standards required for high-risk Singapore commercial tenancies and banking sectors. PowerSeries Neo panels report to Singapore central monitoring centres via a range of IP and cellular communicators, providing the dual-path redundancy mandatory for commercial insurance compliance. The brand's focus on backwards compatibility ensures that Securevision can maintain and upgrade legacy DSC systems with modern communicators without requiring a complete hardware overhaul.

Securevision Scope

Securevision installs the full DSC PowerSeries Neo and PowerSeries Pro range, including zone expansion modules, keypads, and the complete line of PowerG wireless detectors. We provide system design, installation, and multi-year maintenance for enterprise clients across Singapore.

Why We Carry DSC

Engineered for Enterprise Resilience

Securevision specifies DSC (Digital Security Controls) for large-scale commercial and industrial projects where system uptime and long-range wireless capabilities are mission-critical.

🏗️

True Enterprise Zone Scaling

The PowerSeries Pro panel is designed to handle up to 248 zones on a single panel with deep partition capabilities. For Singapore industrial estates and multi-tenanted commercial blocks, this allows for discrete security management of multiple tenancies from a single redundant controller.

📡

PowerG Long-Range Wireless

PowerG technology provides up to 2km of line-of-sight range — significantly more than standard wireless systems. In Singapore warehouse environments with heavy racking and concrete floors, PowerG penetrates structural interference where other wireless signals fail, reducing the need for repeaters.

🏦

Banking and Government Compliance

DSC panels are globally recognized for meeting the stringent security requirements of the banking and government sectors. In Singapore, specifying DSC ensures that your intrusion detection system will pass the technical audits required for high-risk commercial and institutional tenancies.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

DSC PowerSeries Neo panels report to central monitoring via IP and 4G dual-path — when an intrusion zone triggers, the monitoring centre receives the alert and the corresponding CCTV timestamp is bookmarked automatically, allowing for immediate verified video review.
