# Securevision Premises Security: Visual & UI Strategy Summary

## 1. The Dual-Standard Framework
To balance professional storytelling with high-speed scannability, use two distinct image ratios across the site:

*   **Square (1:1 | 800 × 800 px):** Reserved for **Navigation & Solution Cards**[cite: 2]. This creates a uniform grid that prevents vertical "stretching" and ensures a clean UI on mobile and desktop[cite: 2, 3].
*   **Rectangular (3:2 or 16:9 | 1200 × 800 px):** Reserved for **Case Studies & Portfolio Items**[cite: 2, 5]. This wider frame is necessary to show architectural context and environment, such as a landed home or industrial site[cite: 2, 5].

## 2. Integrated Visual Concept: "The Active Perimeter"
For **Premises Security**, the imagery must communicate proactive detection rather than passive recording[cite: 4].

### Non-Residential (Industrial/Commercial)
*   **Hero Asset:** A wide cinematic shot (16:9) of a Singapore industrial perimeter at dusk[cite: 2].
*   **Composition:** Subject hardware on the far right; architectural "safe space" on the left for H1 headers[cite: 2].
*   **Card Image (1:1):** A "Technical Node" showing a professional assembly of a Hikvision/Hanwha camera mounted alongside an industrial PIR sensor with clean white trunking[cite: 1, 4].

### Residential (GCB/Landed)
*   **Hero Asset:** A high-end GCB driveway during Golden Hour, showing discreet perimeter protection[cite: 2].
*   **Card Image (1:1):** A "Macro Rule" crop of a sleek Aiphone intercom or a discreetly mounted camera under eaves[cite: 1, 2].

## 3. UI/UX Redesign Standards
To prevent cards from becoming overly long and overwhelming, apply these standards:

*   **Prose Standard:** Max 1–2 sentences. Content must be outcome-oriented (e.g., "99%+ accuracy on Singapore plates")[cite: 2].
*   **Progressive Disclosure:** Use **Group Tags** (badges) like "AI Analytics" or "Burglar Alarm" at the bottom of the card for rapid scanning of technical scope[cite: 3, 4].
*   **Hierarchy:** H3 Title (2–5 words) followed by prose. All elements must be **left-aligned**[cite: 2].
*   **Grid Logic:** Use an "Equal Height" grid where the primary CTA (e.g., "Explore →") is always pinned to the bottom of the card regardless of text length[cite: 3].
