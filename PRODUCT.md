# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Key decision-makers in Singapore looking for high-quality, compliant security engineering:
- **MCSTs & Managing Agents**: Planning and executing condominium security upgrades (intercom retrofits, LPR vehicle barriers, estate CCTV).
- **Property Developers & Architects**: Specifying M&E security systems, conduit routing, and network infrastructure for new-builds or major renovations.
- **Commercial & Industrial Facility Managers**: Securing office spaces, warehouses, logistics hubs, and data centres with face recognition and fire-integrated locks.
- **Premium Landed Residential Homeowners**: Demanding seamless, reliable auto gate, video intercom, and alarm integrations.

## Product Purpose
To serve as an authoritative, high-trust marketing and educational resource showcasing Securevision's deep engineering expertise, licensing compliance, and successful track record. The website drives business growth by generating inbound requests for site assessments, quotes, and project consultation.

## Brand Personality
- **Engineered & Authoritative**: Rooted in physics, physical systems, and engineering standards. Heavy on expertise.
- **Structured & Clear**: Information-rich, organized, and clean. No hand-wavy marketing copy.
- **Trustworthy & Compliant**: Emphasizing certifications (bizSAFE Level 3, licensed PLRD installation) and historical reliability (since 2006, 2,000+ sites).

## Anti-references
- Avoid superficial "SaaS-cream" or sand/beige minimalist templates that feel generic and thin.
- Avoid aggressive dark "cybersecurity" or "hacker" visual designs; physical estate security requires physical trust.
- Avoid side-stripe borders on callouts, gradient text, or tiny tracked uppercase eyebrows before every section.

## Design Principles
1. **Document-Grade Typography**: Treat pages like highly readable, beautifully typeset technical documents. Use a structured hierarchy, balanced line lengths (65-75ch), and clear font pairing.
2. **Technical Density Over Fluff**: Embrace tables, bulleted specifications, and structured side-by-side comparisons. Do not hide information for the sake of "minimalism".
3. **Direct Paths to Action**: Keep site assessment request forms, WhatsApp inquiry shortcuts, and phone details visible, accessible, and contextually relevant.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Contrast Integrity**: Ensure a minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio for all body copy and placeholder text.
- **Motion Restraint**: Use animations only to clarify physical transitions or system logic; provide instant/reduced-motion alternatives.
- **Structured Hierarchy**: Ensure clean heading structure (one `<h1>` per page) and semantic markup for screen readers.
