About ZKTeco

ZKTeco — Global Leader in Biometric Verification

ZKTeco was founded in 1998 in Dongguan, China, and has become a global leader in biometric verification technology, specialising in fingerprint, face, palm, and iris recognition hardware. The company manufactures a vast range of security products, from simple standalone card readers to multi-modal biometric terminals and integrated security management software. ZKTeco is unique in its breadth of product, offering solutions that cater to both budget-conscious SMEs and complex enterprise environments.

ZKTeco has a dedicated Singapore office and a significant presence in the local market, making it one of the most widely deployed access control brands in Singapore's retail, commercial, and industrial sectors. Their hardware is recognised for its versatility, particularly in time-attendance applications where biometric precision is combined with HR and payroll software integration. ZKTeco terminals are designed to handle high user volumes and are frequently specified for Singapore corporate offices where employee time-tracking is as critical as physical security.

Securevision Scope

Securevision installs ZKTeco biometric readers, time attendance terminals, and access controllers. We specify ZKTeco for commercial and industrial projects in Singapore requiring multi-modal verification and payroll software integration.

Why We Carry ZKTeco

Versatile Access for Smarter Management

Securevision specifies ZKTeco for its unmatched versatility and native time-attendance capabilities, making it the practical choice for Singapore's commercial and industrial sectors.

🖐️

Multi-Modal Biometrics: Face, Finger, Palm

ZKTeco terminals support multiple verification modes simultaneously. In Singapore industries where glove-use or hand-abrasion affects fingerprint accuracy, we specify ZKTeco's contactless face or palm-vein recognition to maintain 99.9% uptime.

💼

SME-Friendly: Professional Features at Scale

ZKTeco delivers professional-grade access control features — such as anti-passback and global interlocking — at a price point that fits SME budgets. Their ZKBioSecurity platform offers a license-free tier for smaller deployments.

⏰

Time Attendance: Native Payroll Integration

ZKTeco terminals are purpose-built for time-tracking. Securevision integrates ZKTeco hardware with local HR/Payroll platforms, allowing employee clock-in data to flow directly into attendance reports without manual calculation.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

ZKTeco readers connect to our elevator controllers for floor-level access — a resident or employee's credential grants lift access only to their authorised floor, with a timestamped log pushed to our VESTA dashboard.
