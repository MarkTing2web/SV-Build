About Aiphone

Aiphone — Reliable Intercom Engineering Since 1948

Aiphone is a Japanese intercom manufacturer founded in 1948 in Nagoya, Japan, and one of the longest-established intercom brands in Singapore's residential and commercial market. The company manufactures audio and video intercom systems across its JO, JF, GT, and IX series product lines — covering single-family residential systems through to large-scale apartment building installations. Aiphone built a strong reputation in Singapore's landed residential and condominium sectors through the 1990s and 2000s, and their systems remain in active service across a significant number of Singapore properties.

Aiphone has had a Singapore distributor presence for decades and their residential and commercial products are well-understood by the local installer community. Spare parts for the JO and GT series are generally available through the distributor network, making maintenance and component replacement practical for existing installations. Their IX series uses SIP protocol, aligning with modern IP intercom standards, though the full SmartPlus cloud management capabilities of Akuvox are not replicated in the Aiphone platform.

Securevision Scope

Securevision has extensive experience installing and maintaining Aiphone intercom systems. For new projects, we now specify Akuvox — which offers IP-native architecture, mobile app integration, and cloud management better suited to modern estate requirements. If you have an existing Aiphone system requiring servicing or expansion, we are fully equipped to support it.

Why Clients Choose Aiphone

Proven Reliability in Singapore Properties

While we specify Akuvox for new IP projects, many Singapore properties continue to rely on Aiphone for its mechanical durability and familiar operation.

🏠

Long Track Record in Singapore Properties

Aiphone has been installed in Singapore's landed homes and condominiums for over 30 years. Many properties have Aiphone systems that continue to function reliably, and homeowners are familiar with the interface — particularly the JO series handset.

🏢

GT Series Handles Multi-Tenant Condo Deployments

Aiphone's GT series was widely specified for Singapore condominium installations through the 2000s and 2010s. The system supports up to 500 tenant units from a single lobby panel with a tenant directory, making it a functional solution for mid-size condominiums.

🌐

IX Series Supports SIP for Modern Integration

Aiphone's IX series uses SIP protocol, allowing door stations to register with third-party SIP servers and IP phone systems. For commercial properties that already invested in IX hardware, it provides a degree of future integration flexibility.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

Existing Aiphone IX series door stations can register with a SIP server alongside other intercom endpoints — for properties planning a phased migration, Aiphone IX stations can continue operating while new Akuvox units are introduced at additional entry points.
