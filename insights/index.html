Our Writing Philosophy

Why We Write These Articles

Most security websites focus on products. We focus on decisions. The articles in this section are based on real questions asked by homeowners, managing agents, facility managers, MCST council members, and business owners throughout Singapore; questions that appear repeatedly across site visits, support calls, and AGM briefings over the past three decades.

Rather than answering the same question one person at a time, we document the answer here so others facing the same situation can benefit from the same experience. A homeowner planning a renovation who understands the cost of not running conduit before the walls close will save more money than any product discount could offer. An MCST council member who understands how condominium intercom tendering works will make a better decision than one who relies entirely on a contractor's recommendation.

These articles are not AI-generated content. Every article is written by Ler Wee Meng from direct project experience. When an article cites a scenario, it is based on a real situation we have encountered, not a hypothetical constructed for search rankings.

Common Questions

About These Articles

1

Are these articles written by AI?

No. Every article in this section is written by Ler Wee Meng from direct installation and project experience. When an article describes a scenario, it is based on a real situation encountered across actual projects in Singapore, not a hypothetical constructed to rank for a keyword.

2

How often are new articles published?

Typically once or twice a month. Articles are published when there is something genuinely useful to document; a recurring question that deserves a thorough answer, an upgrade decision pattern we are seeing repeatedly, or a regulation or technology change that affects how Singapore properties should be thinking about their security systems.

3

Are the articles suitable for non-technical readers?

Yes. Most articles are written for property owners, MCST council members, managing agents, and facility managers who need to understand security systems well enough to make good decisions, not to configure or install them. Technical depth is included where it adds clarity, not to demonstrate expertise.

4

Do the articles recommend specific products or brands?

Articles explain how a system type works and what to consider when choosing one; they do not review or rank specific products. Where a brand is mentioned, it is to illustrate a technology category or a real-world example. Specific brand recommendations for a property are made during a site assessment, not in a general article.

5

Are the articles based on Singapore regulations and practices?

Yes. All articles are written with Singapore-specific context; PDPA obligations for CCTV and access control, PLRD licensing requirements, BMSMA rules for condominium MCSTs, HDB camera placement guidelines, MOM requirements for dormitory operators, and local installation practices. Generic advice that does not apply to Singapore conditions is excluded.

6

Can I suggest a topic for a future article?

Yes. If you have a question about security systems in Singapore that you cannot find a clear answer to, WhatsApp us directly. Questions that appear more than once across multiple contacts are the most likely candidates for a future article; they represent gaps in the available information that are worth filling properly.
