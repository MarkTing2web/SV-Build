CORE POSITIONING: Institutional projects are defined by stakeholder complexity, public accountability, and operational continuity for users who cannot be inconvenienced (commuters, students, patients, citizens). The challenge always starts with the public-service mission first - who must be served, who must be protected, and who is accountable when something fails. Securevision is rarely the head contractor on these projects. We work under main contractors (e.g. W'Ray, civil/M&E specialists) and report against tender specifications issued by government agencies (LTA, MOH, MOE, JTC, CPF, etc.).
