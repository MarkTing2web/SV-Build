CORE POSITIONING: Healthcare projects are defined by duty of care first, security second. Residents and patients are the people the system serves: never the threat the system guards against. The challenge always starts with the care environment: who lives here, who works here, and how does the building support both groups safely without making the place feel like a prison. This template applies to: - Nursing homes and aged-care facilities - Special-needs residential care (intellectual disability, autism, dyslexia support) - Hospitals and clinics - Hospices and palliative care - Mental-health residential facilities - Dementia and memory-care facilities The brief is almost always: protect residents (some of whom may wander, some of whom may be vulnerable to outside threat), support staff (who need accountability and quick response), and respect dignity (no language or imagery that treats residents as suspects).
