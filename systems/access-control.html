The Evolution of Entry

From Keys to Intelligent Access

Physical keys are a security liability. Digital identity management transforms the door from a barrier into a data-rich control point.

Traditional Lock & Key

  • Physical keys can be lost, duplicated, or stolen
  • Static permissions—requires manual key change
  • Zero visibility into who entered or when
  • Manual management of key sets is inefficient

Intelligent Access

  • Digital identity via face, mobile, or cards
  • Dynamic permissions updated in real-time
  • Full audit trails of every movement
  • Centralized management of all entry points
Operational Reality

How Access Control Works in Real Life

See how modern identity management secures high-traffic properties.

Smart Visitor Entry

Visitor is granted access via a mobile credential sent to their phone, integrated with the intercom system.

Staff Movement Logistics

Staff access is restricted by specific time windows and zones, ensuring people are only where they need to be.

Unauthorized Entry Attempt

The system detects a forced entry or use of a revoked card, triggering an instant alert and local area lockdown.

Remote Delivery Access

Property managers can remotely unlock specific doors for verified deliveries while monitoring via integrated camera.

System Capabilities

What This System Enables

Identity Verification

Seamless entry via face recognition, mobile Bluetooth/NFC, or secure card technologies.

Access Scheduling

Define precisely when certain groups or individuals can access specific areas.

Full Audit Trails

Every entry, exit, and denied attempt is logged with a timestamp and identity record.

Remote Access Control

Unlock doors or revoke credentials instantly from a centralized dashboard or mobile app.

Anti-Passback Logic

Prevent credentials from being 'shared' by requiring an exit record before the next entry.

Lift & Zone Control

Manage vertical movement and restrict access to specific floors for residents or tenants.
