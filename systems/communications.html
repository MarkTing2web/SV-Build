The Upgrade Case

From Keyphone to IP — What Actually Changes

Most offices in Singapore still run on keyphone systems installed 10 to 20 years ago. They work — until they don't. IP phone systems solve the underlying limitations rather than patching around them.

Legacy Keyphone System

  • Proprietary hardware — replacement parts increasingly unavailable
  • Physical wiring to every desk — inflexible when layouts change
  • No remote or mobile access — staff must be at their desk
  • Call logs and voicemail require local hardware to access
  • Expanding the system means buying more proprietary extensions
  • Maintenance contracts are expensive and vendor-locked

IP Phone System

  • Industry-standard SIP protocol — not locked to one vendor
  • Runs over your existing network — no dedicated phone cabling
  • Mobile app extends your desk extension to any device, anywhere
  • Cloud or local management — call logs and voicemail accessible remotely
  • Adding extensions is a software licence — not a hardware purchase
  • Open ecosystem — mix handset brands to match your budget
Operational Reality

How IP Phone Systems Work in Real Life

These are the scenarios our office clients encounter — and how an IP phone system handles each one differently from a keyphone.

Staff Working Remotely

A sales manager working from home receives calls to their office extension on their mobile — the caller sees no difference, and the manager does not need to give out a personal number.

New Staff Onboarding

A new hire's extension is provisioned in minutes via the Yeastar management portal — no engineer visit, no hardware order. The handset auto-configures when plugged into the network.

Office Relocation

The office moves to a new floor. IP phones are unplugged, brought to the new location, and plugged into the network there — the extension numbers follow without any reprogramming.

Reception & Auto-Attendant

An IVR auto-attendant routes incoming calls to the right department — press 1 for sales, press 2 for support. Outside hours, calls route to voicemail or a mobile fallback automatically.

Our IP Phone Stack

The Components We Specify and Install

An IP phone system has three layers — the PBX server, the handsets, and the network. We handle all three.

Yeastar IPPBX

The brain of the system. Yeastar P-Series and S-Series IPPBX appliances manage all extensions, call routing, IVR, voicemail, and reporting. Available as a local hardware appliance or cloud-hosted.

  • P-Series — unified communications with app integration
  • S-Series — SME-focused, simple management portal
  • Supports SIP trunks from any local telco
  • Mobile app (Linkus) included — iOS and Android

Fanvil & Yealink Handsets

SIP handsets across a range of form factors — from basic single-line desk phones to executive models with colour screens and programmable keys. Both brands offer strong value at their respective tiers.

  • Fanvil — cost-effective, robust, wide model range
  • Yealink — mid-to-high tier, strong video phone range
  • Auto-provisioned via Yeastar — plug and play
  • PoE powered — no separate power adaptor required

Network Infrastructure

IP phones run on your data network. If the existing switches are not PoE-capable or lack the capacity for voice and data on the same network, we supply and configure what is needed as part of the installation.

  • Omada and Ruijie PoE managed switches
  • VLAN configuration to separate voice and data traffic
  • QoS settings to prioritise call quality
  • Structured cabling if additional points are required
