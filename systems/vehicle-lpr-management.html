The Evolution of Entry

From Manual Logging to Automated Flow

Manual visitor logging is the primary cause of peak-hour congestion. Intelligent recognition automates the gantry, reducing guard intervention and visitor wait times.

Manual Guard Logging

  • Guards manually record licence plates and purpose
  • Significant delays during peak hours (8am/6pm)
  • Handwritten logs are difficult to audit or search
  • Unauthorised vehicles depend on guard alertness

Automated Recognition

  • LPR cameras recognise plates in under 1 second
  • Seamless entry for residents and pre-registered visits
  • 100% digital logs with searchable timestamps
  • Auto-blocking of blacklisted vehicles/visitors
Operational Reality

How Vehicle Access Works in Real Life

See how intelligent barrier management improves property security and traffic flow.

Resident Drive-In

Resident vehicle is automatically recognised via LPR or UHF tag; barrier opens instantly without any guard interaction.

Pre-Registered Visitor

Visitor arrives with an e-invite; the LPR system verifies the plate against the invite list and grants entry automatically.

Time-Limited Taxi Access

Taxis are granted entry for drop-offs, with the system logging their entry/exit times and flagging overstays.

Peak-Hour Congestion Relief

Automated lanes handle 90% of traffic, leaving guards free to manage complex deliveries and exceptions manually.
