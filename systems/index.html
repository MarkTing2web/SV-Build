The Cohesion Layer

How These Systems Work Together

Security becomes effective when systems respond together—not separately.

Detection
Camera detects unusual movement

AI analytics identify potential unauthorized entry at the perimeter.

Trigger
Access system registers forced entry

The system cross-references the detection with the physical door status.

Response
Nearby doors lock automatically

Coordinated lockdown prevents the intruder from moving deeper into the facility.

Alert
Platform sends notification to management

Real-time push alerts and live video streams are sent to the security team.

Review
Incident recorded with full timeline

Management reviews the synchronized timeline of events for audit and reporting.
