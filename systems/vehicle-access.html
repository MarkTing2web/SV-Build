The Philosophy

Vehicle Access: Authorisation by Purpose

Vehicle access control isn't just about installing boom gates — it's about architecting a system that identifies vehicles accurately and authorises entry based on their relationship with the property.

Authorisation strategy: separating authorised users from visitors.

We approach vehicle security through three fundamental questions: Who is the driver? What credential does the vehicle carry? And how do we prove it? Modern systems use License Plate Recognition (ANPR) and IU Readers to provide touchless, high-speed entry while maintaining a 100% digital audit trail.

Two Distinct User Groups

Our design architects separate paths for authorised vehicles (residents, employees, fleet) and temporary visitors. Authorised vehicles require high-speed, frictionless entry, while visitors require verification via intercom or pre-registration apps like VESTA™.

The Business Case

Why Automated Credentials Matter

Manual gate control and audio-only intercoms create significant security gaps. When a guard opens a barrier based on voice alone, there is no record of the vehicle plate or the driver's face.

Technical Anatomy

Six Primary Credential Technologies

We deploy standard Singapore vehicle identification technologies based on traffic volume, budget, and site requirements.

1

ANPR (License Plate Recognition)

AI-powered cameras read Singapore plates mid-stride. This eliminates the need for physical stickers and provides a permanent photographic record of every vehicle that enters the site.

2

IU Tag Readers (ERP System)

Reads the In-Vehicle Unit (IU) tags already installed in every Singapore vehicle. Best for season parking and properties requiring alignment with LTA gantry standards.

💡 Securevision Recommendation

For modern condominiums and commercial hubs, we recommend a hybrid system: ANPR for visitors (to log plates) and IU/RFID for residents (for guaranteed fast entry during peak hours).

System Integration

Vehicle Access + CCTV Unification

A barrier is just a gate until it's connected to your surveillance platform. Integration allows for "Video Verification" — the system automatically matches the recognized plate with a live camera feed to ensure the vehicle model and color match the database.

  • Blacklist Alerts: Notification on entry of unauthorized vehicles.
  • Driver ID: Capture face photos linked to every plate record.
  • Unified Audit: One search finds the plate, the owner, and the video of the entry.
