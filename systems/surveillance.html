Passive vs Intelligent

From Recording to Real-Time Detection

The biggest vulnerability in traditional security is the reliance on human eyes to monitor screens. Intelligent detection shifts the burden to the system.

Traditional CCTV

  • Records footage but requires manual review
  • Relies on guards watching screens 24/7
  • Increases workload and human error
  • Useless until after an incident occurs

Intelligent Detection

  • Detects, analyses, and alerts automatically
  • Reduces reliance on manual monitoring
  • Flags specific anomalies (loitering, intrusion)
  • Enables proactive response before escalation
