About HID Global

HID Global — The Enterprise Benchmark for Credential Security

HID Global was founded in 1991 and is now a part of the ASSA ABLOY group, serving as the world's leading manufacturer of secure identity solutions. Their products form the backbone of access control systems in the majority of Fortune 500 companies, government agencies, and educational institutions globally. HID's expertise lies in the development of encrypted card technology (such as iCLASS and Seos) and the hardware readers that interface with them, ensuring that the critical link between a user's credential and the access controller is secure from cloning or tampering.

HID is the enterprise standard in Singapore, specified for nearly all Grade-A commercial offices, regional headquarters, and sensitive government facilities. Because many multinational corporations use HID credentials across their global offices, HID is the natural choice for Singapore projects requiring international compatibility. Their readers are engineered for extreme durability and are frequently specified for Singapore's high-traffic commercial building lobbies where reliability across millions of transactions is a non-negotiable requirement.

Securevision Scope

Securevision specifies HID card readers and credentials as part of integrated access control systems. We pair HID readers with Suprema or ZKTeco controllers — combining HID's credential security with a full-featured access management platform.

Why We Carry HID Global

Elite Credential Security for Enterprise Projects

Securevision specifies HID Global for projects where credential integrity and backward compatibility are the primary drivers of the security design.

🔄

Multi-Tech Support: Prox, iCLASS & Seos

HID Signo readers support legacy 125kHz Prox cards alongside modern 13.56MHz iCLASS and Seos credentials. For Singapore offices migrating away from insecure legacy cards, HID allows a phased upgrade without replacing every user's credential on Day 1.

🛡️

Seos Ecosystem: Highest Level of Encryption

The Seos platform uses advanced cryptography to protect against card cloning and "man-in-the-middle" attacks. We specify Seos for Singapore's government and high-security enterprise clients where data privacy is a boardroom-level concern.

📱

Mobile Access: Smartphones as Credentials

HID Mobile Access allows users to use their smartphones (NFC or Bluetooth) as security credentials. For Singapore's "Digital First" offices, this eliminates card-loss costs and simplifies onboarding for short-term contractors or visitors.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

HID readers interface with our Suprema controllers via Wiegand or OSDP — combining HID's industry-standard credential security with Suprema's advanced logic for anti-passback and site-wide lockdown.
