About MicroEngine

MicroEngine — Biometric & Card Access Engineering Since 2000

MicroEngine is a Malaysian access control manufacturer that has been distributed in Singapore's commercial and industrial sectors since the early 2000s. The company produces card readers, fingerprint readers, access controllers, and time attendance terminals under the ME series product range. MicroEngine built a presence in Singapore's SME market and light industrial sector as a cost-competitive alternative to more expensive biometric access control brands, and their systems are found across a range of Singapore commercial, warehouse, and factory premises.

MicroEngine's product range covers basic proximity card readers through to fingerprint and face recognition terminals, managed through their ME platform software. The brand is distributed in Singapore through a local network and spare parts are generally available for current product lines. However, MicroEngine's fingerprint algorithm accuracy and management software capability lag behind current Suprema and ZKTeco offerings, particularly for high-traffic commercial environments where rejection rates and software integration requirements have increased.

Securevision Scope

Securevision has installed and maintained many MicroEngine access control systems across Singapore. For new installations, we now recommend Suprema or ZKTeco — which offer superior biometric accuracy, more intuitive management software, and a cleaner upgrade path as your property's needs evolve. If you have an existing MicroEngine system, we can service it or advise on migration.

Why Clients Choose MicroEngine

Practical Biometric Access Management

While we recommend Suprema or ZKTeco for new projects, many Singapore industrial operators continue to run MicroEngine systems for their established performance and familiar platform.

🏭

Established in Singapore's Industrial Sector

MicroEngine has a long track record in Singapore's industrial sector, where their systems were specified during a period when biometric access at this price point was not widely available. Many operators continue to run systems that adequately perform their core function.

☝️

Fingerprint Time Attendance at Competitive Cost

MicroEngine terminals were widely adopted in Singapore's logistics sector for biometric attendance recording under the Employment Act. For sites focused on time attendance rather than high-security access, working terminals continue to serve their purpose.

📋

Familiar Platform for Existing Operators

MicroEngine's ME management software is understood by facility managers who have operated it for years. Database management and cardholder administration are familiar tasks that reduce day-to-day friction for sites not planning a near-term upgrade.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

MicroEngine controllers output Wiegand data that can be connected to CCTV systems for basic timestamp cross-referencing. For migration to Suprema or ZKTeco, Securevision can manage the cardholder data export process, minimising disruption.
