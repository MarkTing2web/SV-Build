How it works

One simple visitor journey

Everyday access should be intuitive, even where there is no guard at every entrance.

  1. 1

    Visitor calls

    The visitor selects the unit at the entrance intercom.

  2. 2

    Resident answers

    The resident sees and speaks to the visitor on a compatible mobile app, at home or away.

  3. 3

    Access granted

    The resident unlocks the designated entrance remotely after confirming who it is.

Expecting guests? Where the system supports it, residents or management can issue a time-limited QR credential so visitors can enter without a call. Features depend on the equipment selected, network connectivity and integration.

After you enquire

What happens next

A clear path from first conversation to a working system, without disrupting residents.

  1. We review your answers

    An engineer looks at your estate size, current system and priorities before contacting you.

  2. Site assessment

    A walkthrough of the property with our engineer. It is free, with no obligation.

  3. Options and costs

    What can be kept, what should be replaced, upfront and ongoing costs side by side, and phasing options. We can provide an assessment report and cost estimate in a format suitable for council presentation.

  4. Phased installation

    The old system stays active while new hardware goes in, block by block for larger estates. We confirm the timeline for your estate in the proposal.

  5. Handover and resident onboarding

    Training for guardhouse and management teams, and a resident communication plan that starts before installation, not after.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Do residents need an intercom monitor in every home?
No. Residents can receive calls and open doors from the mobile app. For households that prefer one, an in-home monitor can be offered as an option, depending on the system selected.
Can our existing intercom wiring be reused?
Often yes. Many modern IP intercoms can run on existing 2-wire or Cat5 cabling. Whether that is feasible depends on cable condition, distances and the system chosen, so we assess the wiring during the site survey before recommending any recabling.
What happens to residents during the upgrade?
We phase the work so the old system stays active while new hardware is installed, and for larger estates we proceed block by block. Residents are told in advance when their unit changes over, and we plan the changeover so guard coverage is not interrupted.
Is it a one-time cost or a subscription?
It depends on the system. Some mobile app platforms involve a licence or ongoing service fee, while other architectures do not. We explain upfront and ongoing costs side by side so you can choose with the full picture.
Can visitor management, lifts or vehicle entry be added?
Yes. Visitor management, lift access and licence plate recognition can be integrated now or in a later phase, as long as the infrastructure is planned to support them.
I am a resident. Can I enquire?
You are welcome to. Decisions on shared systems rest with the MCST council, so the managing agent or council is usually the right starting point. We are happy to speak with you, your managing agent or your council together.
