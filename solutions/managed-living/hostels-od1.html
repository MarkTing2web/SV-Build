The Reality

Managing Shared Living Spaces Is Complex

Hostels and co-living spaces face the unique challenge of managing a high turnover of diverse residents while maintaining the security of shared amenities and private units.

Shared facilities

Ensuring only authorised residents can use gyms, lounges, and laundries.

Frequent visitor movement

Managing the flow of non-residents without creating bottlenecks.

Mixed user types

Balancing access for short-term guests, long-term tenants, and facility staff.

Turnover of residents

Rapidly issuing and revoking access credentials as guests arrive and depart.

Where Traditional Access Falls Short

Why Legacy Security Systems Don't Work for Managed Living

Physical keys and standalone locks create an administrative challenge in high-turnover environments.

Lost keys and cards

High cost of rekeying and the administrative burden of replacing lost credentials.

Poor tracking

No clear record of who accessed facilities or when visitors left the site.

Manual processes

Security guard time is wasted on manual registration instead of site visibility.
