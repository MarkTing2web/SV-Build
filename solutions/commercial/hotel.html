The Hospitality Security Challenge

Why Hotels Are Harder to Secure Than Any Other Building Type

A hotel is not a single building — it is a collection of distinct security environments stacked on top of each other. The lobby is a public space. The guest corridors are private residential zones. The F&B and banquet areas are semi-public event spaces. The back of house is operational. A system that treats the whole building the same will fail in every zone.

🏨

Guests Are Strangers

Hotels welcome thousands of strangers every year. Unlike offices where every person is a known employee, a hotel's guest population is anonymous — creating access control challenges that require guest-specific credential management integrated with the PMS.

🔄

Staff Turnover Is High

Hospitality has some of Singapore's highest staff turnover rates. Every departure is a credential revocation event. Every new hire is a credential issuance event. Manual card management at this volume is an operational risk.

🏢

Multiple Zones, Multiple Rules

Lobby, pool, gym, restaurant, function rooms, back of house — each area needs different access rules by role, time, and credential type. A single flat access system creates operational bottlenecks.

🚗

High-Volume Vehicle Access

Hotel car parks process hundreds of vehicles daily — guests, staff, deliveries, and event attendees. Manual barrier management is a bottleneck at peak times and a liability at quiet ones. Automated LPR with guest pre-registration eliminates both problems.

📹

100+ Cameras to Manage

A mid-size Singapore hotel typically requires 80–150 cameras covering lobby, corridors, car park, F&B, and perimeter. Without an enterprise VMS with smart search and AI analytics, retrieving footage for an incident investigation takes hours not minutes.

🔗

Integration With PMS and BMS

Hotel security does not operate in isolation — it needs to connect with the Property Management System for guest credential sync, with the Building Management System for access-linked lift control, and with the fire panel for emergency door release. Standalone systems that cannot integrate create operational blind spots.

Integrated Systems

A Security Architecture Built Zone by Zone

We design hotel and hospitality security around the zones of the property. Each zone gets the camera coverage, access rules, and integration it requires.

📹

Enterprise Surveillance — 80 to 500+ Cameras

For large Singapore properties, we deploy Hikvision or Hanwha enterprise platforms supporting 80 to 500+ cameras across all zones. AI-powered smart search finds relevant footage in seconds — searching by face, vehicle, or time. 60–90 day storage with RAID redundancy and organisations remote viewing.

🔐

Multi-Zone Access Control With PMS Integration

Suprema or ZKTeco controllers manage access across lobby doors, lift floors, pool, gym, and back of house. For hotels, we integrate the access control platform with the PMS: guest check-in automatically activates room floor access and amenity credentials. Lost keycards are revoked instantly.

🚗

LPR Car Park & Vehicle Management

FAAC boom barriers with LPR cameras automate vehicle access for registered hotel guests, season parking holders, and staff vehicles. Pre-registered guest vehicles are recognised on arrival and the barrier opens without ticketing friction. Visitor and delivery vehicles are managed through an intercom lane with CCTV documentation. The system logs every vehicle entry and exit with timestamp and plate — providing the audit trail required for MAS TRM compliance and insurance purposes.

🚨

Emergency & Fire Integration

Securevision integrates access control with SCDF fire panel output — all electronically controlled doors fail to the unlocked position on fire alarm activation, meeting SCDF egress requirements. The security dashboard displays triggered fire zones alongside corresponding CCTV feeds, giving the duty manager a visual of the affected area before dispatch. Emergency notification pushes alerts to the security team's mobile devices for immediate response coordination.

Our Hotel Security Process

From Site Survey to Live System

Large-facility deployments require more planning than a standard installation. We manage the process in four stages to minimise disruption to your guests.

1

Facility Survey & Zone Mapping

We walk the property with your Facilities Manager — mapping every camera position, access point, lift connection, and integration requirement across all zones.

2

Zone-by-Zone Design & Proposal

We produce a zone-by-zone camera layout, access control configuration, and integration specification with a phased installation schedule.

3

Phased Installation

Staged installation (floor by floor or zone by zone) with night shifts for guest corridors. We coordinate directly with your PMS vendor for integration testing.

4

Commissioning, Training & Handover

Full system commissioning across all zones. Security team training, full documentation package, and ongoing maintenance with priority response.
