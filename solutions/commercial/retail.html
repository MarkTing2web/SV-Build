Solution #1: Stop Employee Theft

37% of shrinkage comes from your own team—deter theft through accountability systems

Employee theft isn't always malicious. Sometimes it's sweethearting, stockroom theft, or POS manipulation. The goal isn't to spy, but to create accountability systems that deter theft while treating your team with respect.

CCTV at Critical Points
  • Point of Sale (POS) stations
  • Stockroom entrance/exit
  • Loading dock (delivery fraud)
  • Cash handling areas
  • Remote viewing capability
Access Control for Stockroom
  • Keycard entry logs (who, when)
  • Restrict access to authorized staff
  • Audit trail for discrepancies
  • Integrate with time attendance
  • Remote access revocation
AI-Powered POS Analytics
  • Flag unusual void transactions
  • Detect excessive discounts
  • False refund detection
  • Under-ringing alerts
  • Pattern analysis across shifts

The deterrence effect: Just knowing cameras are present reduces employee theft by up to 50%. It's not about catching every incident—it's about creating an environment where theft becomes too risky.

Solution #2: Reduce Shoplifting

28% of shrinkage comes from external theft—visible deterrence is your first line of defense

Visible CCTV Deterrence
  • Cameras at entrance
  • High-traffic aisle coverage
  • Signage: "You are being recorded"
  • Monitor visible to customers
  • Color night vision for after-hours
AI Loitering Detection
  • Alerts when person lingers 10+ min
  • High-value item zone monitoring
  • Unusual movement patterns flagged
  • Real-time notifications to staff
  • False alarm filtering
Facial Recognition
  • Flag known shoplifters on entry
  • Alert sent to staff mobile device
  • Watch list management
  • Evidence for police reports
  • PDPA-compliant storage

After-Hours Protection:

Burglar alarm systems detect break-ins, smash-and-grab attempts, or unauthorized after-hours entry. Motion sensors, door contacts, and glass-break detectors trigger instant alerts. Optional 24/7 monitoring service dispatches security or police immediately.

Solution #3: Footfall Analytics

Security cameras that do double-duty—protect your store AND provide actionable retail insights

Modern security cameras are powerful business tools. The same cameras protecting against theft can also count customers, track dwell time, and analyze traffic patterns to optimize store operations.

People Counting
  • Track visitors by hour/day/week
  • Identify peak hours for staffing
  • Conversion rate tracking
  • Compare store performance
  • Non-intrusive counting
Zone Heatmaps
  • Which aisles get most traffic
  • Identify dead zones in layout
  • Optimize product placement
  • A/B test marketing displays
  • Visual overlay on floor plan
Dwell Time Analysis
  • Time spent in each zone
  • Engagement metrics for displays
  • Queue wait time management
  • Staff allocation optimization
  • Marketing effectiveness

ROI Example: Identify peak traffic hours → Reallocate shifts to match → Improve customer service → increase conversion by 5% → Analytics pays for your entire security system.

Comparative Analytics

Real-Time Alerts

License Plate Recognition (LPR):

If your stores have carparks, LPR cameras automatically read vehicle plates and flag known offenders. Data is shared across all stores to track organized retail crime groups across multiple locations.

Retail Security FAQ

Q: Can CCTV really reduce employee theft?

A: Yes. Visible cameras are a primary deterrent—employees who know they're recorded are 50% less likely to steal. CCTV also provide essential evidence for investigating shrinkage when it occurs.

Q: Footfall analytics vs POS data?

A: POS only tells you who bought. Footfall tells you who walked in but didn't buy, giving you conversion rate data to help you optimize marketing, staffing, and store layout.

Q: Do I need License Plate Recognition (LPR)?

A: Only for stores with carparks concerned about repeat offenders. Valuable for retail chains to share offender vehicle data across multiple locations. Not needed for walk-in shops.

Q: How much does shrinkage actually cost me?

A: Industry average is 1.62% of revenue. For $500k revenue, that's $8,100/year. Reducing this by 50% saves $4,000+, often covering the system cost in the first year.

Q: Can you integrate CCTV with my POS?

A: Yes. Integration overlays POS data on video, allowing you to instantly see what happened on camera during void transactions, refunds, or discounts.

Q: Multiple stores — cloud or standalone DVR?

A: Cloud is far superior for chains. Standalone systems require visiting each site or using multiple logins. Cloud VMS gives you one login to manage and compare all stores instantly.
