The Problem

Visitor Management Breaks Down When Everything Depends on Manual Checks

Many properties still rely on guards to verify vehicles, answer intercom calls, log visitors, and decide who can enter. That creates delays at the gantry, inconsistent handling, and incomplete records — especially during busy periods, shift changes, or after hours.

The result is not just inconvenience. It is a weaker visitor process with less visibility and less control.

⏳

Bottlenecks at the entrance

guards checking vehicles manually, queues at peak periods, and inconsistent handling of different visitor types.

📉

Poor visitor visibility

incomplete records, fragmented paper logs, and weak accountability for who is actually on-site.

🧩

Fragmented visitor flow

vehicle visitors handled one way, pedestrians another, with no unified logic or control across the estate.

Why It Fails

Paper Logs, Manual Checks, and Guardhouse Decisions Do Not Scale Well

Traditional visitor management depends heavily on human judgement and repeated manual effort. Guards are expected to:

  • Ask who the visitor is here to see
  • Record names and vehicle details manually
  • Decide whether entry is allowed per unit
  • Handle taxis, pickups, and casual drop-offs
  • Respond to pedestrian intercom calls
Over time, this creates slower entry, higher workload, inconsistent records, and operational disputes. Adding more manpower does not solve the root issue.

The Operational Reality:

  • ❌ Inconsistent Enforcement: Rules change between shifts.
  • ❌ Data Gaps: Missing or unreadable paper records.
  • ❌ Resident Frustration: Long queues to enter own home.
  • ❌ Scalability: Manual systems fail as visitor volume grows.

How It Works

A Smarter Visitor Flow for Vehicles and Pedestrians

The goal is not just to automate entry. It is to make every visitor interaction more controlled, more traceable, and less dependent on manual intervention.

GantryGo Vehicle Access Logic

GantryGo manages the vehicle entry flow for residents, registered visitors, pickups, and short-stay arrivals. It recognises resident licence plates, captures visitor details, and logs arrivals automatically.

  • LPR cameras at entry and exit
  • GantryGo visitor workflow
  • Resident whitelist database
  • Visitor capture and audit logs
📅

Workflow Simulation

Resident Pre-Invite and Approval Logic

Residents can pre-register expected visitors through a mobile interface. Approved vehicles or guests move through the entrance with less friction while remaining under estate control.

  • Pre-registration workflow
  • Visitor validity window
  • Resident-linked approvals
  • Configurable estate rules

Pedestrian Verification and Resident Contact

Visitors can intercom residents directly. Depending on the setup, residents may allow entry via remote release, temporary PIN, or QR-based invitation sent to the visitor's phone.

  • IP intercom at pedestrian entrance
  • Resident directory or dial logic
  • Mobile answering capabilities
  • QR / PIN-enabled visitor access

Better Records and Accountability

Vehicle and pedestrian entries are linked to digital audit records. The estate gains a clearer, searchable history of who entered, when, and under what access path.

  • Vehicle entry logs with LPR data
  • Visitor identity and contact capture
  • Intercom event history
  • Searchable digital audit records
