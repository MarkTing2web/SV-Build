The Problem

Visitor Management Breaks Down When Everything Depends on Manual Checks

Many properties still rely on guards to verify vehicles, answer intercom calls, log visitors, and decide who can enter. That creates delays at the gantry, inconsistent handling, and incomplete records — especially during busy periods, shift changes, or after hours.

The result is not just inconvenience. It is a weaker visitor process with less visibility and less control.

Bottlenecks at the entrance

Guards checking vehicles manually, queues at peak periods, and inconsistent handling of different visitor types.

Poor visitor visibility

Incomplete records, fragmented paper logs, and weak accountability for who is actually on-site.

Fragmented visitor flow

Vehicle visitors handled one way, pedestrians another, with no unified logic or control across the estate.

Why It Fails

Paper Logs, Manual Checks, and Guardhouse Decisions Do Not Scale Well

Traditional visitor management depends heavily on human judgement and repeated manual effort. Guards are expected to:

  • Ask who the visitor is here to see
  • Record names and vehicle details manually
  • Decide whether entry is allowed per unit
  • Handle taxis, pickups, and casual drop-offs
  • Respond to pedestrian intercom calls

Over time, this creates slower entry, higher workload, inconsistent records, and operational disputes. Adding more manpower does not solve the root issue.

The Operational Reality:

  • ❌ Inconsistent Enforcement: Rules change between shifts.
  • ❌ Data Gaps: Missing or unreadable paper records.
  • ❌ Resident Frustration: Long queues to enter own home.
  • ❌ Scalability: Manual systems fail as visitor volume grows.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear from property managers and MCSTs evaluating visitor management upgrades.

Can visitors pre-register before they arrive, or do they have to register at the gate?

Both flows work. Residents can pre-register their visitors through the VESTA resident app or a web portal — the visitor receives a QR code before they arrive and scans it at the guardhouse terminal on arrival. Walk-in visitors who have not been pre-registered are handled through the standard intercom flow, with the guard logging the visit manually. The pre-registration flow reduces guardhouse time per visitor from 45–60 seconds to under 10 seconds.

Does the system work for delivery riders and food delivery as well as personal visitors?

Yes. We configure a separate delivery flow — riders scan a QR code at the guardhouse, select the unit they are delivering to, and the resident receives an instant notification. The resident can confirm delivery remotely and the rider proceeds to the delivery point. No guard interaction required for routine deliveries. All delivery visits are logged with timestamp, unit, and rider identity.

What happens to the visitor log data — who can access it and for how long?

Visitor logs are stored in VESTA and accessible only to authorised estate management staff — the managing agent and designated MCST council members. Individual residents can see their own visitor history but not other residents' visitors. Logs are retained for a configurable period, typically 90 days, and can be exported for council reporting or incident investigation. All data handling complies with PDPA requirements.

How do you handle visitor management at a property with no guardhouse — just an intercom at the gate?

For gateless or guardless properties, we implement a self-service visitor kiosk at the entry point — residents grant access remotely from their mobile device when the visitor arrives. The kiosk logs the visit with a photo capture and timestamp. For properties transitioning from no security to managed visitor flow, this is often the starting point before a more comprehensive system is considered.
