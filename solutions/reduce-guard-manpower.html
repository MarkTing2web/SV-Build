The Problem

The Cost of Manpower is Rising — But the Workflow Remains Manual

Security manpower costs in Singapore continue to climb, yet many properties still rely on fragmented, manual systems that create operational bottlenecks and inconsistent enforcement.

Manual Bottlenecks

Guards checking IDs, manual gate logs, and physical paperwork creates delays during peak hours and high guard house turnover.

Fragmented Systems

When CCTV, intercoms, and vehicle gates don't talk to each other, guards become the "integration layer," leading to human error and fatigue.

Rising TCO

Adding more guards to solve security gaps leads to a spiraling Total Cost of Ownership without actually improving site safety metrics.

Why It Fails

Why Traditional Approaches No Longer Work

Simply hiring more security staff is no longer a sustainable strategy for Singapore's property market. Manual checks are inconsistent and create friction for residents and visitors alike.

Paper-Based Logging

Physical logbooks are slow, difficult to audit, and offer no real-time visibility into who is currently on-site or why.

Static Monitoring

Basic CCTV without AI requires constant human attention — which is physically impossible for guards also managing gate access.

Our Approach

Redesigning the Security Workflow

We don't just add equipment; we help you redesign the entire operational workflow. By automating the routine, we allow your security team to focus on what matters.

A Unified Platform Layer

We believe that true efficiency comes from a unified platform (VESTA) that connects surveillance, access, and visitor management into a single operational dashboard.

This allows for remote verification, self-service visitor registration, and automated vehicle entry — reducing the physical workload at the guard house by up to 40%.

How It Works

Integrated Automation Hub

Our manpower reduction solutions combine proven technologies working together to eliminate manual intervention.

AI CCTV Analytics

Automated human and vehicle detection triggers alerts only when necessary, eliminating the need for constant monitor-watching.

LPR Vehicle Automation

High-accuracy License Plate Recognition automates entry for residents and pre-registered visitors, keeping gates moving smoothly.

VESTA Visitor Management

Replace paper logs with QR-code visitor registration. Residents grant access via mobile app, bypassing the guard for routine entries.

Remote Operations Center

Centralize monitoring for multiple entry points or clusters of blocks, allowing a single guard to manage a much larger area.
