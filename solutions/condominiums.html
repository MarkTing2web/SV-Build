Condominium Security Context

The Reality of Managing a Condominium Today

Condominium security is no longer just about guarding boundaries. It is a complex operational challenge involving rising manpower costs, growing resident expectations for convenience, and the need for absolute accountability. Fragments of legacy hardware often create blind spots that increase friction for residents and workload for guard teams.

Fragmented systems create blind spots

When intercoms, CCTV, and vehicle gantries don't talk to each other, guards are forced into manual workarounds. Integration isn't a luxury; it's the only way to ensure 100% visibility across the estate.

Estate operations must stay smooth

Security should not be a bottleneck. From seamless visitor entry to rapid incident review, your systems must support the daily flow of the estate, reducing complaints and increasing management efficiency.

Security must support daily living

A modern estate should feel open to residents and closed to threats. We design systems that empower residents with mobile control while maintaining the strict audit trails that MCST councils require.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear regularly from MCST councils and managing agents considering a security upgrade.

Can you upgrade our estate without disrupting residents?

Yes — phased installation is standard practice for us on occupied estates. We sequence work to ensure security remains active throughout, typically starting with back-of-house infrastructure before moving to resident-facing touchpoints. We also communicate directly with your managing agent on timeline and access requirements.

Our estate has ageing analogue systems. Can you integrate with them or do we need to replace everything?

It depends on the condition and age of the existing infrastructure. In many cases we can bridge analogue systems into a digital backbone using IP converters, extending their useful life while adding modern functionality. For systems beyond serviceable life, we design phased replacement plans that spread costs across the MCST budget cycle.

What audit trail documentation do you provide for MCST AGMs and council reviews?

Our VESTA platform generates access event logs, incident reports, and system health records that are exportable for MCST council review. All reports are timestamped and named-individual attributed — meaning the council can demonstrate exactly who accessed what and when, which is increasingly important for insurance and liability purposes.

How do you handle LPR and vehicle access when residents have multiple vehicles or change cars?

LPR systems allow multiple plate registrations per unit, and plates can be updated by your managing agent through the platform dashboard — no need to call us for routine changes. Visitor vehicle management is handled separately through a visitor pre-registration or intercom-triggered temporary access flow, depending on your guardhouse setup.

What happens if the system goes down after installation — what is your response time?

Our maintenance contracts include a 4-hour response SLA for critical faults via our VESTA remote monitoring platform, which alerts our team before most issues are even noticed on-site. For non-critical issues, same-day or next-business-day response is standard. We also provide 24/7 emergency contact for gate or access failures that affect resident movement.

Can the system send alerts to our managing agent and the MCST chairman directly?

Yes. VESTA supports configurable alert routing — specific incident types can be sent to designated contacts via push notification, email, or SMS. You can set different alert levels for different recipients, so the guardhouse gets real-time alerts while the MCST chairman receives a daily summary report rather than individual notifications.
