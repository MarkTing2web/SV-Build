Visibility Gaps

Common Gaps That Limit CCTV Effectiveness

This is exactly what many sites face before a visibility performance upgrade.

Blind Spots

Areas not covered due to poor camera placement or outdated site layouts that have changed since installation.

Low Light Performance

Footage becomes unclear, monochrome, or completely black in dim conditions or night environments.

Narrow Coverage

Requiring too many cameras because each covers a limited field of view, creating gaps in continuity.

Footage Retrieval

Wasting hours of staff time searching for events because the recorder lacks smart indexing.

The Risk

When Visibility Is Limited, Response Becomes Reactive

When footage is unclear or hard to access, your security posture weakens significantly. CCTV should reduce uncertainty, not create more of it.

  • Incidents are significantly harder to verify or prove
  • Operational response is delayed by lack of real-time info
  • Guards are forced to rely heavily on manual patrol vs tech-enabled monitoring
  • Investigations become time-consuming and often inconclusive
⚠️

The Uncertainty Gap

If your current system cannot identify a person or a license plate at night, it is providing a false sense of security.

Strategic Outcomes

What a Visibility-First System Should Deliver

Eliminate Blind Spots

Cover critical areas with proper planning, mapping angles effectively to ensure continuous coverage across access routes.

  • Site-based placement planning
  • Digital coverage mapping
  • Optimized vertical/horizontal angles

See Clearly Even in Low Light

Maintain visibility in night and challenging conditions using ColorVu or DarkFighter technology that keeps footage in full colour.

  • Colour imaging in pitch black
  • Reduced false alarms at night
  • Lower reliance on expensive floodlighting

Wider Coverage, Fewer Cameras

Improve efficiency with wide-angle panoramic cameras that cover entire lobbies or open spaces with a single device.

  • 180°/360° panoramic views
  • Reduced maintenance points
  • Fewer gaps between camera transitions

Faster Video Search

Smart recorders with AcuSense technology allow you to find what you need in seconds without reviewing hours of video.

  • Human/Vehicle event filtering
  • Timeline heatmapping
  • Instant thumbnail navigation

AI Video Analytics

Detect and highlight what matters. The system helps the operator notice unusual activity automatically.

  • Intrusion detection lines
  • Abandoned object recognition
  • Loitering alerts for high-risk zones
