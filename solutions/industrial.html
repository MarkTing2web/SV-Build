Compliance Gaps

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we regularly hear from facility managers and operations directors evaluating industrial security upgrades.

Can your LPR system handle the throughput of a high-volume logistics hub?

Yes. Our GantryGo LPR configurations are rated for continuous high-cycle operation and can process multiple vehicles simultaneously at multi-lane gantry setups. Recognition speed is under 500ms per plate in normal conditions. We size the system — cameras, lane controllers, and barrier cycles — against your actual peak-hour vehicle count during the site assessment.

How do you manage access for contractors and third-party drivers who visit frequently?

We configure three credential tiers: permanent credentials for employees and regular contractors, time-window credentials for delivery drivers and service contractors, and visitor access via guard-authorised intercom for one-off visitors. LPR handles regular vehicles automatically. All access events are logged with timestamp and vehicle or personnel identifier.

What WSH compliance documentation can your system generate?

The VESTA platform generates access event logs, incident records, and zone-entry reports exportable for WSH officer review. For facilities with PPE compliance requirements, AI camera analytics can flag non-compliance events with timestamped footage. We provide documentation of the installed system configuration for your WSH safety management system records.

Our facility operates 24/7. How do you install without disrupting operations?

We phase installation around your operational schedule — typically starting with infrastructure cabling and back-of-house systems during lower-activity windows, then commissioning gantry and access systems in planned maintenance windows. For critical gantry points, we maintain manual backup access during the switchover. Full-site installations are typically completed within 5–7 operational days.

We have multiple sites across Singapore. Can you manage them all from one platform?

Yes — multi-site management is a core VESTA feature. All sites are visible from a single dashboard with per-site and aggregate views. Access credentials can be managed centrally — an employee's card works across all sites they are authorised for, and changes propagate instantly. Incident alerts surface to the designated contact regardless of which site they originate from.

How do your cameras perform in high-humidity outdoor environments and direct sunlight?

We specify IP67-rated cameras with wide dynamic range (WDR) for outdoor industrial installations. WDR handles the contrast between direct sunlight and shaded loading bay interiors — a common failure point for consumer-grade cameras in Singapore's conditions. Housings are rated for continuous outdoor exposure and are covered under our maintenance contracts for the duration of the agreement.
