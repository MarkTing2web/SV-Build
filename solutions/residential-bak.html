Operational Methodology

The Securevision Home Protocol

Our methodology for private residences focuses on "Discreet Governance"—ensuring that your security is absolute while the hardware remains physically unobtrusive and the logic remains intuitive for the family.

  • 1
    Primary Identification — Encrypted biometrics and mobile intercom logic for family and staff.
  • 2
    Perimeter Logic — AI-driven detection that differentiates between family movement and actual boundary breaches.
  • 3
    Unified Control — Single-platform management of access, surveillance, and alerts via VESTA™.
