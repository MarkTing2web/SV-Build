The Problem

Vehicle Access Is Often Slower Than It Should Be

In many properties, vehicle entry still depends on guards manually triggering barriers, residents waiting for access, and inconsistent handling of different vehicle types.

During peak periods, this leads to congestion at entrances, delays for residents, and increased pressure on security personnel who are tied up with repetitive tasks.

Operational Friction

Manual barrier operation creates an average delay of 15-30 seconds per vehicle. At scale, this causes significant tailbacks.

The Friction Points

Common Issues in Vehicle Access Points

This section highlights the operational realities of non-automated entry points.

Manual Operation

Guards manually opening and closing barriers for every vehicle significantly slows down total site throughput.

Peak Hour Congestion

Morning and evening traffic peaks create bottlenecks that spill over into public roads and cause resident frustration.

Inconsistent Handling

Confusion and delays caused by different rules for residents, registered visitors, taxis, and heavy deliveries.

Verification Delays

Vehicles waiting at the gantry while access is manually confirmed via intercom or phone call to residents.
