Sector Challenges

Why Guarding Models Need To Evolve

Guarding companies in Singapore are facing unprecedented operational pressures. Balancing rising labour costs with client expectations for high service quality requires a shift towards technology-augmented operations.

Manpower Pressure

Progressive Wage Model (PWM) updates and rising labour costs are compressing margins across the industry.

Contract Pricing

Resistance to higher contract pricing from MCSTs forces companies to find new ways to maintain service standards with less manpower.

Manual Workflow Risks

Over-reliance on manual visitor logs and gantry operation increases the risk of human error and security gaps.

Quality Expectations

Clients increasingly expect real-time site visibility and digital audit trails as part of standard service delivery.

Strategic Support

Where Technology Can Support Guard Teams

Integrated technology is not about replacing guards; it's about empowering them to focus on high-priority security tasks while automating the repetitive ones.

Visitor Verification

Self-service visitor kiosks and QR code access reduce the burden of manual registration at the main gate.

Vehicle Automation

Advanced License Plate Recognition (LPR) allows for automated entry of known vehicles, freeing guards for patrol.

Incident Review

AI-powered surveillance allows for rapid incident retrieval and remote review, supporting guardhouse teams in real-time.

Guard Workflow Integration

Connected systems provide guards with immediate data on their monitors — from intercom calls to perimeter alerts.

Operational Outcomes

From Manual Work To Better-Controlled Operations

We help guarding companies transition to outcome-based site support, where technology provides the necessary auditability and responsiveness to stay competitive.

Improved Responsiveness

Faster incident detection via smart sensors and AI cameras allows guard teams to react before a complaint is even filed.

Increased Visibility

Command-and-control dashboards provide management with a clear view of site security status across multiple estates.

Auditability

Every visitor, vehicle, and incident is digitally logged, providing the "proof of service" required by modern MCSTs.

Commercial Flexibility

Flexible Deployment Thinking

We understand that guarding contracts are finite. Securevision offers flexible deployment models that allow you to adapt technology to the site and the contract term.

Reduce Capital Exposure

Low-upfront deployment options that help you manage cash flow across building site launches.

Adapt Between Sites

Scalable hardware that can be redeployed or expanded as contract requirements change over time.

Contract Term Alignment

Support models designed to sync with your service lease, ensuring hardware remains relevant until renewal.
