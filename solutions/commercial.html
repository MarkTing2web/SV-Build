Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear regularly from business owners and facilities managers evaluating commercial security upgrades.

Can you install security systems in our office without disrupting daily operations?

Yes — we plan installations around your business hours. For offices and retail environments, we typically work in phases: cabling and back-of-house infrastructure first, then customer-facing or staff-area components during lower-traffic periods. We coordinate directly with your facilities team on access and scheduling.

We have multiple outlets or branches. Can you standardise security across all of them?

Yes — multi-site consistency is a core part of what we do for retail chains and office portfolios. Our VESTA platform gives you centralised visibility across all locations from a single dashboard, while each site's access control, surveillance, and alarm systems operate on a standardised configuration that simplifies maintenance and staff training.

What does PDPA compliance mean for our CCTV system and how do you address it?

PDPA requires that surveillance footage is stored securely, access is restricted to authorised personnel, and footage is not retained beyond what is necessary. We configure NVR systems with role-based access controls, automatic retention schedules, and encrypted storage. We also provide documentation of the system configuration for your PDPA compliance records.

Can your access control system integrate with our existing HR or visitor management software?

In many cases yes, depending on the software involved. Our access control platforms support API integration with common HR and visitor management systems, allowing employee onboarding and offboarding to automatically update access credentials without manual intervention. We assess integration feasibility during the initial site consultation.

How do you handle access for contractors, cleaners, and other third-party staff?

We configure time-limited access credentials for contractors and third-party staff — typically tied to specific zones and time windows. Credentials expire automatically at the end of the contract period and can be suspended instantly if needed. All access events are logged with the individual's credential, giving you a full audit trail of third-party movement on-site.

What ongoing support do you provide after installation?

We offer maintenance contracts that include proactive remote monitoring via VESTA, scheduled preventive maintenance visits, and a defined response SLA for faults. For retail and hospitality clients, we also provide staff training refreshers when there is turnover in the team responsible for system operation. You'll have a named point of contact, not a generic helpdesk number.
