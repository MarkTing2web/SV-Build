Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear regularly from architects, interior designers, and main contractors evaluating Securevision as a security subcontractor.

At what stage of the project do you need to be engaged?

The earlier the better — ideally at or before structural drawings are finalised. This allows us to advise on conduit routes, cable access points, and device positions before walls close. For projects where we join at fit-out stage, we work with what the existing infrastructure allows and flag any compromises in writing so your client understands what could not be achieved versus an early-engagement specification.

What documentation do you provide for M&E coordination?

We provide a pre-wiring schedule in the format your M&E contractor requires — cable types, conduit specifications, conduit routes, device locations, and power point requirements. We also produce as-built drawings on completion, system manuals, zone lists, and a handover package for your client. If you need device positions marked on your floor plans, we produce a marked-up PDF layer you can incorporate into your drawing set.

How do you handle the aesthetic requirements of high-end residential projects?

We treat device placement and hardware aesthetics as part of the design brief, not an afterthought. We select camera housings, intercom stations, and reader hardware that match the design intent — surface finishes, form factors, and colour temperatures. We do not install visible cable trunking on finished surfaces without your agreement. If a concealment approach requires a structural or joinery solution, we flag it during design stage rather than improvising on-site.

What is your liability position if a system fault occurs after handover?

We carry full public liability and professional indemnity insurance. Our workmanship warranty covers defects in installation for 12 months from commissioning. Equipment warranties are passed through from the manufacturer — typically 2–3 years depending on the brand and product. We provide full warranty documentation at handover. If a fault occurs after the warranty period, we provide maintenance and repair services at agreed rates under a service contract or on a time-and-materials basis.
