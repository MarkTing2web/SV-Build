Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Questions we hear regularly from architects, IDs, and contractors evaluating our partnership.

At what stage of the project do you need to be engaged?

The earlier the better: ideally at or before structural drawings are finalised. This allows us to advise on conduit routes and device positions before walls close. For projects already underway, we work with the existing infrastructure and flag any technical compromises in writing.

What documentation do you provide for M&E coordination?

We provide a pre-wiring schedule including cable types, conduit specifications, and power point requirements. On completion, we deliver as-built drawings, system manuals, and a handover package. We can also provide marked-up PDF layers for your architectural drawings.

How do you handle the aesthetic requirements of high-end projects?

We treat device aesthetics as part of the design brief. We select hardware finishes and form factors that match your intent, and we ensure no visible cable trunking is installed on finished surfaces without your prior agreement.

What is your liability position and warranty coverage?

We carry full public liability and professional indemnity insurance. We provide a 12-month workmanship warranty from commissioning, while equipment warranties (typically 2–3 years) are passed through from manufacturers.
