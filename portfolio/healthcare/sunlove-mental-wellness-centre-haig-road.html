The Care Context

Supporting Wellness Through Thoughtful Design

Sunlove's Haig Road facility is designed as a therapeutic space where residents can focus on their wellness journey. Daily life involves a mix of structured activities, communal dining, and quiet rest. The primary goal of any security system here is to support this routine without making the residents feel restricted or under constant observation.

Staff need to maintain a high level of operational awareness to ensure resident safety, particularly in common areas and at exit points. Managing the transition between public-facing zones and staff-only areas-such as medication rooms and administrative offices-requires a reliable access strategy that prevents unauthorised resident entry while ensuring staff can move freely.

The security need at Sunlove was to provide a "quiet guardian" system. This means 100% visibility for incident review and accountability, coupled with an access control layer that hardens secure zones without introducing complexity for the care team.

Decision Point

Designing for Supervised Freedom

A critical design decision for the Haig Road facility was how to manage the main entrance and lift lobby. The operator needed to ensure that residents could move safely within the facility without the risk of unsupervised egress into the busy Haig Road area.

Securevision recommended a two-tier access strategy. We integrated the MicroEngine platform with the lift controllers to ensure that residents can only access their designated floors, while staff maintain full override capability for all zones using a single credential.

This decision balanced the residents' need for movement with the staff's duty of care, creating a facility that feels open but remains fundamentally secure.

Vertical Access Control

Simplified Incident Review

Trained the care management team on rapid footage retrieval to support MSF reporting and internal care planning.

By focusing on these four pillars, we delivered a system that enhances the care experience for everyone at Sunlove.

Dignity is a Design Requirement

In a mental wellness setting, security must never feel like a barrier to care. Our role is to provide staff with "eyes" in common areas and "lock-and-key" control over staff zones, all while ensuring the residents feel respected in their home. The best care-facility security is the one that residents notice through their sense of safety, not through the presence of hardware.
