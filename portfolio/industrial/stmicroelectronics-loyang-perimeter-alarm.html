The Industrial Reality

Securing 1.2 Kilometres of Exposure

The primary challenge for the STMicroelectronics Loyang facility is its scale. With a 1.2km perimeter bordering industrial roads and adjacent foliage, the site is vulnerable to environmental false alarms. High winds, heavy rain, and local wildlife frequently triggered legacy PIR sensors, leading to "alarm fatigue" where genuine intrusions could be missed by a desensitised guard team.

In a high-value semiconductor environment, unverified alarms are a significant operational risk. Each alarm requires a manual patrol to verify the cause, diverting security resources and creating windows of vulnerability. The industrial reality of this site demanded a system that could not only detect an intrusion but immediately verify it visually for the control room.

Furthermore, the long-distance cabling required for a 1.2km perimeter necessitates industrial-grade network infrastructure that can deliver consistent power and data across the entire site without signal degradation in harsh weather.

Decision Point

A Phased Zonal Architecture

The key engineering decision at Loyang was how to segment the 1.2km perimeter. A single large zone would be impossible to manage, as an alarm would not provide enough detail on where to look. Conversely, too many zones would overcomplicate the management platform.

Securevision designed a 32-zone logical architecture, with each zone corresponding to a specific camera field of view. We recommended an integrated RISCO-Hikvision bridge that allows the alarm panel to command PTZ cameras to instantly "slew-to-cue" upon a zone breach.

This approach transformed the security operation from a blind search to a directed response, significantly reducing the time-to-verification for the site's security team.
