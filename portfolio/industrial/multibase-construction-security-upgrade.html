The Industrial Reality

Engineering Reliability for the Tuas Heartland

The Multibase facility in Tuas South is a harsh operating environment. Constant dust from steel fabrication, vibrations from heavy machinery, and extreme heat during the day rule out standard commercial-grade security hardware. Any system installed here must be industrial-rated to prevent premature component failure.

Operationally, the yard faces a constant flow of prime movers and heavy trailers delivering raw materials. Manual gate logs were causing significant delays and were prone to human error, creating a bottleneck that impacted the yard's production schedule.

Furthermore, the 24-hour nature of the yard meant that shift changes occurred in low-light conditions, requiring a surveillance system with high-sensitivity sensors that could classify events accurately without the guard team suffering from alarm fatigue.

Decision Point

Prioritising Heavy-Duty Infrastructure

The key decision for Multibase was whether to reuse existing domestic-grade gate infrastructure or invest in a full industrial-grade replacement. While the initial cost of industrial barriers is higher, the high frequency of vehicle movement in a fabrication yard causes domestic motors to burn out within months.

Securevision recommended a full transition to Dormer industrial barrier arms with high-cycle-rated motors. This was coupled with an AI-first surveillance strategy that used hardware with IP67 and IK10 ratings to withstand the dust and impact risk of the yard.

This "Industrial-First" strategy has resulted in zero hardware downtime since commissioning, even during peak production periods.
