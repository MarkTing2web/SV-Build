Client Overview

About STA Inspection

STA Inspection Pte Ltd (formerly STA Inspection) is Singapore's trusted LTA-authorised vehicle inspection centre, operating for over 40 years. Their two purpose-built inspection centres - at Jalan Boon Lay and Sin Ming - run continuous multi-lane vehicle inspection operations, handling everything from routine roadworthiness checks to type approval inspections and ERP OBU installations.

The nature of their operations presents a dual security requirement: robust premise security to protect staff, assets, and premises after hours, alongside a comprehensive operations monitoring capability to maintain process integrity and accountability across busy inspection lanes during operating hours.

What This Partnership Represents

STA's security architecture illustrates how a high-throughput operational facility needs two distinct system layers: premise security for protection, and process monitoring for accountability. This separation of purpose - and the engineering to deliver both reliably - is a pattern applicable to any busy commercial or industrial operations site.

Integrated Premise Security with Video Verification

A major security upgrade for the Jalan Boon Lay centre, consolidating camera coverage, perimeter alarm protection, and office access control into an integrated premise security architecture - with a key innovation in alarm-linked video verification.

Completed November 2019

Key Engineering Feature

15 of the 31 cameras deployed are RISCO-integrated video verification cameras. When the perimeter alarm is triggered, these cameras automatically push verification footage to the monitoring channel - allowing security personnel to confirm a genuine intrusion before responding, dramatically reducing false alarm call-outs.

Operations Monitoring System

Lane Operations & Counter Surveillance

A purpose-built operations monitoring system for the busy inspection floor - distinct from the premise security layer above. The focus here is process integrity: continuous, high-retention recording of all inspection lane activity and payment counter transactions.

Completed October 2020

Why RAID + UPS Matters Here

An LTA-regulated inspection centre handles sensitive vehicle data and financial transactions. Three months of video evidence - on RAID storage so no single drive failure loses footage, with UPS ensuring recording never stops during a power blip - gives STA management the ability to review any inspection or transaction retrospectively. This is not a security afterthought; it's an operational compliance and accountability system.
