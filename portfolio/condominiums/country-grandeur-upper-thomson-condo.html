Project Snapshot

Built in 1996, Country Grandeur is a boutique condominium that values its quiet, residential character. Like many estates of its era, it reached a tipping point where legacy security systems had become a source of daily friction for its 68 households.

Client / MCST Country Grandeur MCST
Location Thomson, Singapore
Sector Residential / Condominium
Project Type Intercom Retrofit & CCTV Modernisation
Completion 2025
Units 68 Residential Units
Technologies Implemented
Akuvox 2-Wire IP Intercom panels and indoor monitors. Hikvision HD surveillance cameras and NVR recording systems.
The Challenge

The High Cost of Legacy Friction

For years, the estate relied on a telephony-based intercom system that had become temperamental. For residents, this wasn't just a technical spec-it was a daily inconvenience that compromised the estate's boutique feel.

Operational Friction

  • Failed calls from the gate forced residents to walk down to open gates.
  • No mobile access meant residents were tethered to wall handsets.
  • Guards had limited perimeter visibility during evening hours.
Transformation

From Legacy Friction to Seamless Entry

Feature Before (Legacy) After (Securevision)
Visitor Greeting Audio only, often distorted HD Video + Clear Audio
Resident Mobility Tethered to wall handset Mobile App + Indoor Monitor
Guard Operations Manual logs, blind spots Visual confirmation + HD recording
System Health Frequent downtime Cloud-monitored IP backbone
Decision Point

To Patch or to Progress?

The MCST faced a classic dilemma: continue spending the maintenance budget on "patching" an obsolete system, or invest in a total upgrade? As a boutique estate, budget consciousness was paramount. The council needed certainty that an upgrade would deliver a tangible improvement in resident quality of life.

Our Approach

Designed for Real-Life Reliability

Our approach for Country Grandeur was built on three pillars: Reliability, Usability, and Longevity. We chose the Akuvox 2-wire IP platform to deliver modern, high-definition video capabilities to every unit without the disruption of expensive new cabling throughout the building.

The Solution

A Multi-Layered Modernisation

1. Akuvox R28A Panels

Sleek, weather-resistant IP intercoms at the main entrance providing crystal-clear video for visitor verification.

2. 2-Wire IP Monitors

Installed in every apartment, these monitors provide instant visual control over the estate entrance.

3. SmartPlus App

Giving residents the ability to receive intercom calls and open gates from their phones, anywhere in the world.

4. HD CCTV Network

A new NVR-backed CCTV system covering the perimeter for 24/7 guard visibility.

Results & Impact

Restoring the Flow of Daily Living

✓ Frictionless Resident Experience

The management committee reported a significant drop in access-related complaints from residents.

✓ Operational Professionalism

Security staff now have the visual tools to manage the perimeter with professional precision.

✓ Zero Infrastructure Disruption

The upgrade was completed using existing wiring, preserving the building's aesthetic integrity.
