Project Snapshot

Idyllic Suites required a strategic departure from fragmented, legacy hardware towards a unified digital ecosystem. The goal was to restore estate integrity by closing security gaps in card access, improving visitor accountability, and establishing total visibility over shared spaces.

Client / MCST Idyllic Suites MCST

Project Type System Consolidation & Integrated Modernisation
Completion December 2023

Technologies Implemented
Akuvox Smart intercom panels (X915/R20A) and cloud-ready access points. Hikvision Evidence-grade 180° wide-angle surveillance network.
The Challenge

Identifying Operational Risks

The estate was facing three distinct operational failures that compromised security and resident harmony in a high-density urban environment.

Critical Security Gaps

  • Credential Duplication: Legacy EM cards were easily cloned at local locksmiths, leading to loss of entry control.
  • Zero Traceability: Previous intercom system provided no record of visitor access, leading to protocol abuse.
  • Operational Blind Spots: Insufficient CCTV in the carpark led to indiscriminate parking and frequent disputes.
Transformation

Decision Point

Why Modernisation Was Essential

The MCST council recognised that continuing with patchwork repairs would not solve the underlying loss of control. The decision was made to upgrade into a single integrated system to achieve perimeter integrity, establishing a logged record for every entry event, and establishing total visibility over shared assets.

Our Approach

A Coordinated System Pivot

What were previously fragmented silos of equipment—two intercom systems and three standalone card systems—have now been unified into a single integrated platform. This shift provides centralised control and a singular source of truth for estate management, allowing the council to manage the entire estate security ecosystem from a single cloud dashboard.

The Solution

Integrated Security Infrastructure

1. Flagship Akuvox Intercom

Deployed Akuvox X915 panels for main entries and R20A for staircase access, enabling HD video calls and mobile app answering.

2. Credential Hardening

Migrated to encrypted MiFare protocol integrated into the cloud, eliminating the risk of unauthorised card cloning.

3. Full Visibility Surveillance

Strategic deployment of 180° super wide-angle Hikvision cameras to eliminate coverage gaps in common areas and carparks.

4. Industrial Gate Automation

Replacement with an industrial-grade gate motor fully integrated into the unified access control system.

Results & Impact

Restoring Estate Order
