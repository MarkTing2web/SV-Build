The Challenge

The Fragility of Unreliable Tech

The estate was struggling with a "modern" system that was theoretically advanced but practically broken. The previous facial recognition setup frequently "hung", requiring manual reboots and preventing residents from gaining reliable entry.

Daily access failures had compromised the MCST's credibility. The council faced constant complaints about a "smart" upgrade that had effectively become a barrier to entry, leading to a total loss of confidence in experimental technology.

Restoring Response & Stability

Decision Point

Prioritising Consistency Over Novelty

The Mergui Mansions council faced a critical choice: attempt to fix a fundamentally unstable experimental setup or move toward a proven industrial platform. The turning point was reframing the discussion from "new features" to long-term dependability.

Securevision advised that system instability is not a trait of modern tech, but of unsuitable platform choice. By selecting the Akuvox ecosystem, we promised — and delivered — a foundation that residents could trust for their daily commute.

Our Approach

Stabilisation Through Diagnostics

Instead of a simple like-for-like hardware swap, Securevision performed a deep diagnostic of the site's infrastructure debt. We identified that the estate needed multi-layered access resilience to ensure no single point of failure would lock out residents again.

  • Industrial Stability: Deploying hardware designed for high-traffic tropical environments.
  • Methodical Phasing: Phase 1 focused on entry restoration; Phase 2 on total surveillance modernisation.
  • Education: Working with the MCST to set realistic operational standards for "smart" access.
The Solution

An Industrial-Grade Integrated Ecosystem

Intercom Restoration

Akuvox Smart Access

Replaced failing facial units with R28A video panels. Residents now verify visitors via high-res video calls to their mobile phones with 100% uptime.

Surveillance Backbone

16-Camera HD Network

Modernised the entire CCTV grid using Hikvision HD cameras, ensuring every sensitive zone and entry point is monitored with evidence-grade clarity.

Wide-Angle Visibility

180° Carpark Monitoring

Deployed specialized super-wide cameras to provide seamless coverage of large carpark areas, reducing camera count while increasing total visibility.

Results & Impact

Stability Restored. Trust Rebuilt.

✓ 100% System Uptime

No incidents of system "hanging" or manual reboots reported since the Phase 1 deployment.

✓ Resident Confidence

Restored faith in the estate management by delivering a "smart" system that actually works every day.

✓ Evidence-Grade Audit

The new CCTV grid provides clear visual evidence for all incidents, eliminating previous blind spots.

Securevision Insight

Reliability is the Ultimate Feature

"Advanced features can appear attractive, but without reliability, they create operational risk. A security system is not judged by what it can do, but by how reliably it performs every day. At Mergui Mansions, the priority was restoring that daily confidence."
