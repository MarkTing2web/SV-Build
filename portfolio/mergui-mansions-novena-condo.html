Condominium Case Study 📍 81 Mergui Road, Singapore

Mergui Mansions — Modernising Condo Security for Smarter Living

Recovering from a failed facial recognition experiment to establish a stable, high-authority integrated security backbone.

Property Type: Condominium Project: Failed System Recovery Status: Phase 2 Completed

The Objective

Restoring Reliability and Resilience

Mergui Mansions required an urgent departure from a failed, unreliable facial recognition system that had compromised resident trust. The primary mandate was to rebuild a foundation of operational reliability, transitioning from experimental technology to an industrial-grade integrated ecosystem that would eventually scale into a comprehensive estate-wide surveillance network.

The Real Problem

The Fragility of Unreliable Tech

The estate was struggling with a "modern" system that was theoretically advanced but practically broken, leading to daily operational failure.

A. Unreliable Face Recognition

The previous system frequently "hung" and required manual reboots. Residents were often unable to gain access reliably, leading to a total loss of confidence in the estate's primary entry protocol.

B. Loss of Confidence in Smart Systems

The MCST raised a critical concern: "Is it normal for such systems to hang?" There was growing uncertainty about whether all modern systems were inherently unstable, creating a significant decision risk for the council.

C. Extreme Resident Frustration

Daily access failures had compromised the management's credibility. The council faced constant complaints about a "smart" upgrade that had effectively become a barrier to entry.

The Decision Moment

Prioritising Consistency Over Novelty

Mergui Mansions' initial foray into face recognition was driven by a desire for the latest technology. However, the real-world performance failure proved that "advanced" features are a liability without industrial-grade stability.

The Turning Point: The council moved the discussion away from "features" toward long-term dependability. Securevision reframed the solution, recommending the Akuvox platform not for its novelty, but for its proven performance in high-traffic residential environments.

Expert Clarification: System instability and "hanging" is not an inherent trait of modern systems—it is typically the result of selecting the wrong technology or an unsuitable platform for the high-demand environment.

Industrial-grade Akuvox R28A deployed at the estate back gate.

The Approach

Stabilisation Through Diagnostics

Securevision’s assessment focused on identifying the root cause of the frequent system lock-ups rather than simply proposing a newer version of the same technology. Our approach was guided by two principles: industrial-grade hardware stability and multi-layered access resilience to ensure the estate would never face a total access blackout again.

The Solution

More Than Replacement — A Better System

Securevision did not simply replace the units like-for-like; we upgraded the property's capability from a simple "access reader" to a fully integrated intercom + access platform designed for absolute stability.

Why Akuvox? Securevision recommended the Akuvox platform based on its proven deployment track record in residential environments, stable firmware performance, and strong ecosystem support. Our focus was on delivering consistent real-world reliability rather than experimental novelty.

Phase 1: Intercom Integration

Reliable Visual Response

Replaced failing hardware with Akuvox R28A panels. This transitioned the estate from an unreliable facial recognition system to a stable video intercom platform, allowing residents to verify visitors clearly without the frustration of system lock-ups.

Practical Access Methods

Stable Credential Layer

Equipped residents with Mifare cards, key tags, and a unified mobile app. By providing multiple access methods, the system avoids reliance on a single point of failure, ensuring continuous access even if one method is unavailable.

Phase 2: Visibility Upgrade

16-Camera HD Network

With trust restored in Phase 1, we modernized the entire surveillance backbone. Deployed 16 cameras to eliminate previous blind spots and provide evidence-grade monitoring estate-wide.

Advanced Coverage

180° Carpark Monitoring

Utilised super wide-angle 180° cameras in the carpark. This provided continuous visibility over large areas that previously required multiple cameras, simplifying the network architecture.

Results & Impact

Restoring Response & Governance

✓ Precise System Stability

No incidents of system hanging or required reboots observed since deployment. Residents gain entry reliably every day.

✓ Restored Confidence

The smooth daily access experience has successfully rebuilt resident faith in the property's management.

✓ Simplified Management

Administrators now manage visitor intercom, access credentials, and CCTV surveillance through a unified digital backbone.

✓ Total Visibility

Phase 2 CCTV upgrade has provided the MCST with a clear, 180° visual of previously blind estate areas.

The Engineering Insight

"Advanced features can appear attractive, but without reliability, they create operational risk. At Mergui Mansions, the priority shifted from adopting the latest technology to implementing a system that works consistently. A security system is not judged by what it can do, but by how reliably it performs every day."

From Recovery to Expansion

Trust Built Through Performance

Correcting a failed system rebuilds more than just security—it rebuilds trust. The success of the Phase 1 access restoration was so definitive that the MCST management immediately engaged Securevision for Phase 2: a total CCTV network modernisation.

By proving the stability of the core intercom system, we established the confidence necessary for the estate to invest in 16 HD cameras and super-wide 180° coverage for the carpark, extending site-wide visibility as a standard.
