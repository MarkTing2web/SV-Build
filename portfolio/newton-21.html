The Situation

This project represents a common turning point for Singapore's mid-sized developments: when legacy hardware creates more friction than security.

Newton21 faced the growing gap between their residents' mobile-first lifestyles and an ageing intercom system that required physical presence to manage. The transition wasn't just about new devices—it was about digitising the entire entry experience.

Project Snapshot
Location Newton Road, Singapore
Property Type Private Condominium
Units 69 Residential Units
Timeline October 2025 – January 2026
Key Partners Akuvox, Securevision
Strategic Context

For the MCST at Newton21, the objective was clear: modernise the access experience without a massive structural overhaul. This required a cloud-native approach that could deliver premium features over the existing footprint with minimal resident disruption.

The Challenge

A System Out of Sync with Resident Life

Before the upgrade, Newton21 was managing with an ageing and increasingly obsolete intercom system. The impact was felt daily: residents were tethered to their home handsets to receive visitors, and property guards were bogged down with manual verification for every guest and delivery.

This fragmentation led to significant operational friction:

  • Resident Inconvenience: Inability to grant access while away from home or even in another room.
  • Guard Inefficiency: Constant manual checks for recurring visitors and couriers at the entry point.
  • Limited Oversight: Fragmented systems provided no centralised logs or audit trails for vehicle and people movement.

The Real Impact: Successive equipment failures began to look like an operational risk. The lack of visibility and the inconvenience to residents had reached a tipping point where a "patchwork" repair was no longer sustainable.

The Transformation

A Structured Migration to Smart Living

A. Smart Intercom Upgrade

We replaced the legacy lobby hardware with modern Akuvox intercom panels equipped with facial recognition, RFID, and multi-credential support. This restored high-definition audio and video communication at all 4 lobby entry points.

B. Mobile Access for Residents

All 69 households were onboarded onto a cloud-based mobile app. Residents gained the ability to view live video, speak with visitors, and unlock doors remotely from their smartphones, regardless of their location.

C. Visitor Access Transformation

The introduction of temporary QR codes and PIN-based access allowed residents to pre-authorise guests. This significantly reduced the guardhouse workload by enabling self-service entry for verified visitors.

D. Guardhouse Integration

A centralised IP video phone was installed at the guardhouse, allowing security personnel to coordinate with residents and visitors more effectively through a unified communication interface.

E. Resident Onboarding & Transition

A structured on-site registration exercise was conducted to ensure 100% adoption. Our team provided hands-on support for facial recognition enrolment and app setup, ensuring a smooth transition for all residents.

F. Targeted CCTV Upgrade

Existing surveillance was bolstered with high-clarity IP cameras in key shared areas, providing the management team with improved visibility and motion-triggered alerts.

G. Infrastructure & Deployment

Securevision implemented a robust PoE network and professionally structured cabling while reusing existing locking mechanisms to maintain cost-efficiency and structural integrity.

Decision Point

The Shift Toward Sustainability

The MCST council faced a clear choice: continue paying for frequent repairs on a failing system or invest in a modernised layer that could grow with the estate. The decision to move toward a mobile-first, cloud-connected environment was driven by the need for better control, traceability, and a superior resident experience.

Our Approach

The Strategic Methodology

Securevision's approach to Newton21 was underpinned by four strategic goals:

1. Infrastructure Assessment

We retained all usable cabling and locking hardware to keep the project minimally invasive and cost-effective.

2. Cloud-Based Logic

By utilising cloud servers, we eliminated the need for heavy on-site server maintenance and enabled remote updates.

3. Seamless Integration

We ensured the resident app, lobby intercoms, and guard operations functioned as a single coordinated ecosystem.

4. Onboarding Support

We planned a phased implementation with dedicated on-site support to ensure every household was ready for the switch.

The Results

Operational Restoration & Enhanced Visibility

✓

Remote Resident Control: Residents can grant entry from anywhere, eliminating dependence on physical presence.

✓

Smoother Visitor Entry: QR-based self-service entry has reduced gate congestion and resident frustration.

✓

Reduced Guard Load: Security personnel now focus on monitoring rather than manual access verification.

✓

Auditability & Security: Every entry and vehicle movement is now logged and traceable within the cloud platform.

✓

Future-Ready Infrastructure: The system is ready for AI analytics and further gantry integrations without full replacement.

The Felt Impact

"A successful upgrade is not just about replacing systems—it is about ensuring people adopt and benefit from the change. At Newton21, the combination of mobile access, structured onboarding, and integrated systems enabled a smooth transition from legacy infrastructure to a smarter, more connected living environment."
