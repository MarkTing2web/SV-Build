CONDOMINIUM CASE STUDY Newton Road, Singapore Private Condominium

Newton21

A dual-infrastructure modernisation project: upgrading legacy intercom and access protocols while significantly expanding site-wide CCTV visibility and surveillance control.

The Situation

High-Expectation Living Meeting Ageing Technology

Newton21 faced two interconnected operational hurdles: an ageing intercom system that tethered residents to physical handsets and an outdated CCTV network with significant blind spots. The lack of digital access verification and fragmented surveillance meant that both visitor handling and general estate monitoring had become inefficient and reactive.

The absence of modern audit trails—for both people entry and vehicle movement—left the management team with limited oversight. For guards, the burden of manual verification combined with insufficient camera coverage meant security was often managed through guesswork rather than real-time data.

The Decision

Moving Beyond "Patchwork" Repairs

The MCST council recognised that reactive security was no longer sustainable. They required a dual-pillar modernisation: better control over who enters the development and better visibility over activity across the shared site. The objective was to implement high-authority access and surveillance tools that could grow with the estate while ensuring reliable long-term recording and incident review capabilities.

Our Approach

Engineering a Seamless Pivot

Securevision functioned as a solution partner rather than a simple installer. We began by assessing the existing cabling and locking hardware to identify reusable components, keeping the project cost-efficient. We then designed a cloud-connected ecosystem that integrated resident mobile apps with lobby intercoms and guardhouse operations—carefully planning every implementation phase to ensure 100% continuity for residents.

Strategic Context

For the MCST at Newton21, the objective was clear: modernise the access experience without a massive structural overhaul. This required a cloud-native approach that could deliver premium features over the existing footprint with minimal resident disruption.

The Challenge

A System Out of Sync with Resident Life

Before the upgrade, Newton21 was managing with an ageing and increasingly obsolete intercom system. The impact was felt daily: residents were tethered to their home handsets to receive visitors, and property guards were bogged down with manual verification for every guest and delivery.

This fragmentation led to significant operational friction:

  • Resident Inconvenience: Inability to grant access while away from home or even in another room.
  • Guard Inefficiency: Constant manual checks for recurring visitors and couriers at the entry point.
  • Limited Oversight: Fragmented systems provided no centralised logs or audit trails for vehicle and people movement.

The Real Impact: Successive equipment failures began to look like an operational risk. The lack of visibility and the inconvenience to residents had reached a tipping point where a "patchwork" repair was no longer sustainable.

The Transformation

A Structured Migration to Smart Living

This was not just a system replacement. It was a redesign of how access and visibility work together to support daily operations. By moving communication to the cloud, Newton21 transformed from a development with isolated hardware into a connected community where residents manage their own visitor workflows.

1. Smart Intercom Upgrade: We replaced legacy lobby hardware with modern Akuvox X915 panels equipped with facial recognition and multi-credential support, restoring high-definition visitor verification and modernising the entry aesthetic.

2. Mobile Access for Residents: All 69 households were enabled with a cloud-connected mobile app, allowing residents to answer calls and release doors remotely from any location, eliminating dependence on physical handsets.

3. Visitor Access Transformation: The introduction of temporary QR codes and PIN-based entry allows residents to pre-authorise guests, shifting visitor management to a self-service model and reducing gantry queues.

4. Guardhouse Integration: Centralised IP video phones were installed at the guardhouse to improve coordination between security personnel, residents, and visitors via a unified digital interface.

5. Resident Onboarding & Transition: A structured on-site registration exercise ensured 100% adoption, with our team providing hands-on support for app setup and biometric enrolment from Day 1.

6. CCTV Upgrade & Coverage Enhancement: A total of 32 IP cameras were deployed across key access points, lift lobbies, and shared common areas, supported by a 32-channel NVR with 16TB storage. The system was designed to eliminate blind spots and improve visibility across the estate, allowing guards and management to monitor activity with greater clarity and confidence. With centralised recording and extended storage, incidents can now be reviewed quickly and accurately, strengthening both daily operations and audit capability. Strategic use of wide-angle cameras enabled broader coverage with fewer blind spots, reducing the need for multiple overlapping views.

7. Infrastructure & Deployment: Securevision implemented a robust PoE network and structured cabling while reusing existing locking paths to ensure high-performance reliability.

The Results

Better Control, Stronger Visibility

✓

Modernised Resident Experience: 69 households are now freed from fixed handsets, enjoying global mobile access for visitor verification and lobby release.

✓

Reduced Blind Spots & Improved Coverage: Strategic camera placement provides clearer visibility across key areas, reducing reliance on multiple camera feeds and improving real-time monitoring effectiveness.

✓

Enhanced Site-Wide Awareness: The combination of updated access logs and expanded CCTV coverage provides the council with total situational awareness of all people and vehicle movement.

✓

Faster Incident Resolution: Centralised NVR recording with high-capacity storage allows for rapid footage retrieval and clearer evidence for any potential security or maintenance reviews.

✓

Improved Guard Effectiveness: Security guards now benefit from a unified interface for both intercom communication and site-wide surveillance monitoring, moving from reactive patrols to structured oversight.

✓

Future-Ready Foundation: The IP-based infrastructure is ready for AI-driven analytics and further gantry integrations without structural disruption.

The Insight

A successful upgrade is not about replacing hardware—it is about redesigning how a building operates. At Newton21, integrating access and surveillance transformed both resident experience and daily security operations, moving the estate from reactive management to structured control and visibility.
