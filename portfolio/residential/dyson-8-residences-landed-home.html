Design Intent

Protection Without Intrusion

This development was designed by aKTa-rchitects, a Singapore-based design practice known for contemporary residential and commercial architecture.

In landed homes of this calibre, security cannot be an afterthought. The objective was to create a system that homeowners can rely on—without feeling its presence dominantly within the architecture.

We implemented technology that complements the clean, contemporary design rather than dominating the facade or interior. This creates a protective envelope that supports both privacy and convenience for residents and guests, giving a system homeowners can rely on for full protection while remaining discreet throughout daily living.

Our engineering design standards prioritised preserving clean contemporary architectural lines, ensuring clear perimeter awareness with zero blind spots, implementing intuitive visitor communication channels, and delivering industrial-grade reliability for long-term use.

Our Approach

Planned Early. Integrated Properly.

We worked closely with the project team during the development phase to ensure proper infrastructure planning before completion. By engaging early, we avoided the aesthetic compromise often seen in retrofit security installations.

Strategic placement of devices preserved the architecture while ensuring maximum coverage across access, surveillance, and intrusion detection. This ensured the security was built into the home’s DNA—not added as an afterthought. The final commissioning phase focused on homeowner usability, ensuring that despite the complex technology, daily operation remains intuitive and reliable.

Security Layers

A Layered Security Stack for Modern Landed Living

Each residence is protected by a comprehensive 4-layer stack, providing full coverage from the outer perimeter to the interior sanctuary.

Intrusion Protection

Burglar Alarm

The Paradox burglar alarm system provides internal zones and perimeter contacts, ensuring peace of mind during both day and night.

Surveillance

CCTV Network

8-camera Hikvision CCTV grids eliminate blind spots, covering the driveway, entrance, side access, and rear perimeter.

Visitor Communication

Intercom System

Aiphone audio-video intercoms allow residents to verify visitors clearly from within the home before granting access.

Entry Control

Gate Automation

FAAC auto sliding gates provide secure, motorized driveway entry that integrates with the estate's access protocols.

Outcome

Seamless Protection for Modern Living

The final outcome delivered seamless integration with the architectural design and complete perimeter and interior protection. Homeowners benefit from reliable day-to-day operation and a discreet system that enhances rather than disrupts the living environment.

By designing the systems during the build phase, we achieved a level of technical integrity that retrofitted systems simply cannot match—ensuring the home remains both beautiful and secure.
