CONDOMINIUM CASE STUDY 📍 118 Lorong H Telok Kurau, Singapore

Still Waiting for Visitors to Connect?

Upgrade from delayed, audio-only telephony systems to instant visual access and mobile control.

Property Type: Condominium Project: Telephony Replacement Status: Completed

The Objective

The Verte aimed to eliminate the critical delays and lack of verification inherent in their aging telephony system. The objective was to provide residents with instant visual confirmation of visitors and the ability to manage access remotely from their mobile devices.

The Challenge

The Operational Cost of Blind Access

For years, The Verte relied on a traditional telephony intercom. While familiar, the system created a "blind" experience that compromised both security and daily convenience.

A. Delayed Response

Visitors were frequently left waiting at the gate due to sequential dialing delays. If the first number didn't pick up, the system took seconds to cycle to the next—often leading to missed connections and frustrated guests.

B. No Visual Verification

Residents were forced to grant entry based on voice alone. Without seeing who was actually at the gate, the estate was vulnerable to unauthorised access from anyone claiming to be a delivery person or guest.

C. Operational Inefficiency

Updating tenant details was a slow, manual process. Every move-in or phone number change required an onsite contractor visit, leading to high maintenance costs and slow turnover handling.

The Decision Point

Breaking the "Like-for-Like" Cycle

When the old telephony system reached its end-of-life, the Council initially considered a direct replacement with another telephony unit. There was hesitation about whether a boutique condominium truly needed "luxury" video features.

The Turning Point: The council realised that replace-like-for-like would simply carry over the same delays and security blind spots. By choosing a cloud-managed smart platform, they weren't just buying hardware—they were investing in a faster response, visual verification, and the ability to manage the entire estate without onsite technicians.

The Solution

Engineering Instant Control

Securevision transitioned the estate from a hardware-reliant audio system to a visual-first digital ecosystem.

A. Smart Intercom

Instant Video Response

Replaced the old telephony system with an Akuvox R28A panel. Every visitor call is now an instant HD video stream, allowing residents to verify identity before speaking.

B. Resident Control Layer

SmartPlus Mobility

Residents no longer need to be home to answer the door. They can answer calls, see live feeds, and remotely grant gate entry from their mobile phones from anywhere.

C. Access Control Expansion

Estate-Wide Consolidation

Integrated A02S readers at the Gym and Back Gate. This ensures that resident-only access points are secured under the same unified management platform.

D. Cloud-Managed Admin

Zero-Hardware Maintenance

Administrative updates are now completed remotely in real-time. No more onsite programming or expensive technician call-outs for routine changes.

Measurable Results

Results & Impact

Restoring Response & Precision

✓ Instant Response

Visitors are now connected instantly without waiting for sequential phone line cycles.

✓ Visual Verification

Residents can visually verify every visitor before granting access, significantly hardening estate security.

✓ Remote Administration

Administrative updates for new tenants are completed remotely within minutes, not days.

✓ Operational Independence

Reduced long-term reliance on external contractors for routine system changes and maintenance.

The Engineering Insight

"Telephony systems are familiar but fundamentally outdated for modern estate governance. They introduce unnecessary delays, lack critical visibility, and create administrative friction. The Verte demonstrates that smart intercom isn't just a hardware upgrade—it's a shift in how a property restores control and resident convenience."

Technology Behind This Project

Akuvox

Smart R28A intercom and A02S access terminals managed via the SmartPlus cloud platform.

Securevision Platform

Migration of telephony database to cloud-managed accounts and end-to-end integration of gym/gate access.

SmartPlus Mobile

App-based controls that replace traditional fixed handsets, providing residents with global reach and visual control.
