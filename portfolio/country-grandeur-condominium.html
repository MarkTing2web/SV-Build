The Context

What This Project Represents

Built in 1996, Country Grandeur is a 68-unit boutique condominium that values its quiet, residential character. Like many estates of its era, it reached a tipping point where legacy security systems—once state-of-the-art—had become a source of daily friction.

This project is a masterclass in practical modernisation. It proves that you don't need a massive budget or a 500-unit scale to benefit from high-authority digital security. It was about restoring the simple expectation of a reliable home—where visitors are greeted clearly and residents feel in control, whether they are at the front gate or miles away.

The Challenge

The High Cost of Legacy Friction

For years, the estate relied on a telephony-based intercom system that had become temperamental. For residents, this wasn't just a technical spec—it was a daily inconvenience.

  • Visitor Access Hurdles: Failed calls from the gate meant residents had to physically walk down to open gates or risk missing deliveries.
  • The "Mobile Gap": In an era of online deliveries and flexible working, the inability to manage access from a smartphone was a significant limitation.
  • Guard Limitations: Without modern visual tools, guards had limited visibility of the perimeter, relying on guesswork during evening hours.

The Decision Point

To Patch or to Progress?

The MCST faced a classic dilemma: do we continue spending the maintenance budget on "patching" an obsolete system, or do we invest in a total upgrade?

The Tension: As a boutique estate, budget consciousness was paramount. The council needed to be certain that an upgrade would deliver a tangible improvement in resident quality of life, not just "new hardware."

Securevision was engaged not just as an installer, but as a consultant to design a practical roadmap that utilised the existing wiring where possible, significantly reducing the cost and disruption of the transition.

Our Approach

Designed for Real-Life Reliability

Our approach for Country Grandeur was built on three pillars: Reliability, Usability, and Longevity. We chose the Akuvox 2-wire IP platform specifically because it allowed us to bring modern, high-definition video capabilities to every unit without the need for expensive new cabling throughout the building.

We treated the guardhouse not just as a gatehouse, but as a "Service Hub," giving the staff the tools to see, hear, and respond to residents and visitors with professional precision.

The Solution

The Technical Foundation

Akuvox R28A Panels

Sleek, weather-resistant intercoms at the main entrance providing crystal-clear video for visitor verification.

2-Wire IP Monitors

Installed in every apartment, these monitors provide instant visual control, replacing the old audio-only handsets.

SmartPlus App

Giving residents the ability to receive intercom calls and open gates from their phones, anywhere in the world.

HD CCTV Network

A new NVR-backed camera system covering the perimeter, carpark, and common areas for 24/7 visibility.

Results & Impact

Restoring the Flow of Daily Living

The impact at Country Grandeur was immediate. Beyond the technical specs, the atmosphere of the estate changed. The management committee reported a significant drop in access-related complaints, and for the first time in years, the "entry experience" was seamless.

The Engineering Insight

"Modernisation isn't about complexity; it's about clarity. For Country Grandeur, we didn't just install hardware—we removed friction. When security technology becomes invisible, it's doing its job perfectly."
