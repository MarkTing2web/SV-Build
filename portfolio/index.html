Expert Advisory

What We’ve Learned Across Projects

01

Infrastructure First: Many intercom failures are infrastructure problems (wiring/voltage), not just hardware failures. We always assess the backbone first.

02

The Fragmentation Cost: Fragmented systems often create more manpower burden than expected. Consolidating interfaces usually pays for itself in guard efficiency.

03

Design Over Devices: Better visibility usually comes from better design and angle selection, not just more devices. Strategic placement outweighs raw camera counts.
