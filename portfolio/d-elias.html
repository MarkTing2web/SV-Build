Project Context

D’Elias is a premium cluster townhouse estate where resident expectations for security and convenience are naturally high. However, the development was burdened by aging, disconnected systems that were no longer aligned with modern operational standards or resident lifestyles.

This highlights Securevision's role as a system integrator, moving the property from isolation to total integration.

The Situation

A Fragmented Security Environment

The estate was operating across six independent access behaviours—two intercom systems, three standalone card access systems, and one vehicle remote system. This "siloed" approach created operational inefficiencies and resident frustration.

Standalone Infrastructure Layers:

  • Intercom: Two separate legacy BTicino systems at the front and back gates.
  • Access Control: Three standalone card systems across different pedestrian and hydrant gates.
  • Vehicle Access: The main vehicle gate relied entirely on physical remote controls.

Prior to the upgrade, some units were unable to receive visitors, and residents were using a common PIN code to enter the premises, compromising security.

The Challenge

Risks Beyond Reliability

While system failures were a major concern, the more critical challenge lay in the estate's underlying security architecture.

Operational Friction

No central control meant that management had no unified audit trail or single dashboard to manage estate access.

Security Vulnerability

The reliance on legacy EM cards posed a significant risk. These cards are easily duplicated making it impossible to truly control unauthorized access. In addition, the estate granted a common PIN code which while convenient, created security risk.

The Decision

Integration Over Individual Replacement

The management council recognized that simply replacing "like-for-like" devices would only perpetuate the silos. They made the strategic decision to shift towards a unified platform—one that could bring intercom, pedestrian access, and vehicle operations into a single manageable ecosystem.

The Transformation

Engineered for Consolidation

1. Intercom & Access Integration

Two Akuvox R28A smart panels replaced the legacy BTicino units. By utilizing the R28A's built-in reader, we eliminated the need for separate standalone access hardware at the pedestrian gates.

2. Hydrant Gate Security

An Akuvox A02S access reader was deployed at the hydrant gate, extending the unified security protocol to every estate entry point.

3. Vehicle Gate Control

The vehicle gate, previously dependent on standalone remotes, was integrated into the system—allowing residents to open the gate via mobile app or intercom in addition to their standalone remotes.

System Consolidation Achieved

What was previously multiple independent systems is now managed through a single integrated platform—reducing complexity and long-term maintenance overhead.

Results & Impact

Operational Simplicity Regained

✓ Unified Operational Visibility

The managing agent now has a single point of truth. All gate activity, visitor logs, and system health are managed through a single digital dashboard.

✓ Hardened Estate Security

The migration from EM to MiFare has eliminated the risk of unauthorized card duplication, providing the council with true control over estate credentials.

✓ Frictionless Resident Living

Residents now enjoy a singular access experience. Whether at the pedestrian gate, vehicle entrance, or hydrant gate, they use the same app or secure card.

The Insight

True security is not achieved by adding more systems, but by removing fragmentation. At D’Elias, consolidating intercom, access control, and vehicle entry into a unified platform eliminated vulnerabilities while improving both administration and daily life.
