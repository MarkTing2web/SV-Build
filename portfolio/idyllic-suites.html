CONDOMINIUM CASE STUDY 📍 21 Lorong 30 Geylang, Singapore

From Uncontrolled Access to Full Visibility and Secure Living

Replacing fragmented legacy systems with a unified, high-authority smart platform for integrated intercom, access, and surveillance control.

Property Type: Freehold Condominium Project: System Consolidation Status: Completed

The Objective

Restoring Estate Integrity

Idyllic Suites required a strategic departure from fragmented, legacy hardware towards a unified digital ecosystem. The goal was to restore estate integrity by closing security gaps in card access, improving visitor accountability, and establishing total visibility over shared spaces.

The Challenge

Identifying Operational Risks

The estate was facing three distinct operational failures that compromised security and resident harmony.

A. Extreme Security Risk

Legacy EM cards were easily duplicated at any local key-smith, meaning the estate had effectively lost control over who held valid entry credentials.

B. Zero Accountability

The previous intercom system provided no digital footprints. Management had no record of visitor access, leading to suspected abuse of building entry protocols with no way to verify or trace incidents.

C. Operational Blind Spots

Insufficient CCTV coverage in the carpark led to indiscriminate parking and frequent disputes, with no video evidence to enforce estate by-laws or review incidents.

The Decision Point

Why Modernisation Was Essential

The MCST council recognised that continuing with patchwork repairs would not solve the underlying loss of control. The decision was made to upgrade into a single integrated system to achieve:

  • • Perimeter Integrity: Eliminating bypass-able card protocols.
  • • Audit Trails: Establishing a logged record for every entry event.
  • • Visibility: Achieving real-time oversight of shared property assets.
  • • Future Stability: Consolidating multiple vendors into one high-authority platform partner.
The Solution

A Coordinated System Pivot

What were previously fragmented, silos of equipment—two intercom systems and three standalone card systems—have now been unified into a single integrated platform. This shift provides centralized control and a singular source of truth for estate management.

A. Intercom Modernisation

Flagship Akuvox Deployment

Before: Unreliable handset-based system with zero data logging or remote capability.

Transformation: Deployed 3× Akuvox X915 flagship panels for main entries and 1× R20A for staircase access, enabling HD video calls to mobile apps.

Impact: The X915 platform future-proofs the estate with premium residential features and remote unlocking, while every event is now logged and traceable.

B. Access Transformation

Credential Hardening

Before: Easily duplicated legacy cards with zero encryption.

Transformation: Migrated to encrypted MiFare protocol integrated into the Akuvox cloud. Added 1× A02S access-only point for peripheral security.

Impact: Restore confidence in estate credentials by eliminating the risk of unauthorized card cloning.

C. Surveillance Upgrade

Full Visibility Deployment

Before: Fragmented CCTV with significant blind spots in the carpark.

Transformation: Strategic deployment of 180° super wide-angle cameras to eliminate coverage gaps in common areas.

Impact: Resolved carpark disputes and indiscriminate parking through evidence-grade video clarity and total area monitoring.

D. Gate Automation

Infrastructure Reliability

Before: Aging auto sliding gate motor prone to failure and disconnected from access logs.

Transformation: Replacement with an industrial-grade gate motor fully integrated into the unified access system.

Impact: Established 100% operational uptime with a singular control point for all vehicle and pedestrian entry.

"Smaller developments often lack full-time guard oversight, making them vulnerable to fragmented systems that offer no centralised oversight. At Idyllic Suites, we proved that restoring estate control isn't just about 'installing systems'—it's about restoring accountability through total system integration."
