CONDOMINIUM CASE STUDY 📍 Pasir Panjang, Singapore Residential Estate

“Restoring Convenience Across 148 Homes”

From a failing legacy intercom system causing significant resident inconvenience to a multi-system upgrade that unified access across the entire estate.

What This Project Represents

A transition from reactive component maintenance to a proactive, redesigned access architecture that prioritises resident experience and operational reliability.

The Challenge

A Legacy System Incapable of Modern Demands

Resident Inconvenience & Safety Gaps

The estate's Samsung intercom panels were chronically failing, with several visitor panels completely non-functional. This forced residents — particularly those in units with private lift access — to physically descend to the lobby to escort visitors, creating a massive daily inconvenience.

Obsolete Technology

The existing hardware was obsolete, making replacement parts difficult to source. Furthermore, the system lacked mobile access capability, leaving residents tethered to physical handsets and unable to manage access remotely.

Fragmented Access Layers

The estate's security layers — intercom, lift access, and vehicle entry — were fragmented and managed as separate, uncoordinated systems. This lack of integration led to operational inefficiency and a disjointed experience for both residents and management.

A simple replacement of failing components would not solve the broader issue.

Decision Point

The Strategic Shift

Redesign vs. Replacement

The key question was whether to continue replacing failing components — or redesign access across the entire estate. The council decided on a cohesive redesign to future-proof the development.

Our Approach

Engineering a Unified Access Environment

Securevision treated the multiple access layers as a coordinated system rather than separate installations. We implemented a phased deployment from February to May 2023, ensuring minimal disruption to the 148 households.

Infrastructure Modernisation

We replaced the obsolete Samsung panels with high-reliability Akuvox hardware, expanding functionality to include mobile app integration and cloud-based management.

The Solution

Systems & Equipment Deployed

A multi-layered solution was designed to restore reliability and add modern smart features.

System 01

Smart Intercom

21 Akuvox R28A panels were installed across 9 blocks, replacing the failing legacy hardware and restoring full reliability with 4G/SIP backup.

Learn more about Akuvox →

System 02

Lift Access Control

Akuvox EC32 controllers were integrated across 5 blocks with private lifts, ensuring that visitor access is strictly controlled from lobby to door.

System 03

Vehicle Access (GantryGo)

A GantryGo LPR system was deployed to automate resident entry and improve visitor vehicle handling, reducing congestion at the main gate.

Explore GantryGo tech →

Ongoing Maintenance Commitment

Securevision continues to maintain the intercom, CCTV, and access systems to ensure long-term reliability and system health for the council.

Measured Outcomes

What Changed After Deployment

A failing and fragmented system was transformed into a cohesive and manageable access environment.

100%

Restoration of intercom functionality across all 9 residential blocks.

Zero

Need for residents to escort visitors manually, thanks to mobile and lift integration.

Automated

Vehicle entry with high-accuracy LPR, reducing wait times and manual logging.

The Insight

When systems fail across multiple areas, replacing individual components is no longer sufficient. The real opportunity lies in redesigning how access works across the entire development to create a unified, manageable environment.
