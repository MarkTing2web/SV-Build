Project Overview

Integrated Residential Security for a Tropical Courtyard Home

This contemporary tropical landed residence at Siglap Bank was designed by HYLA Architects. The home turns inward around a central courtyard, creating a private sanctuary of light, air, and calm within a dense urban landed context. The architecture balances openness and privacy, requiring a security design that supports this balance without dominating the experience of the home.

Securevision delivered an integrated residential security system that supports the home’s inward-facing privacy. The scope included intrusion protection, CCTV surveillance, visitor communication, and automated gate control, all designed to remain discreet within the refined architectural setting while providing robust boundary control.

LocationSiglap Bank, Singapore

Security Systems Implemented

1. Burglar Alarm

RISCO LightSYS with 14 zones, providing granular intrusion protection across the property perimeter and internal spaces.

2. Surveillance

Hikvision 16-channel CCTV system with 7 evidence-grade cameras for comprehensive approach point monitoring.

3. Intercom

Aiphone JOS system with 3 indoor monitors, enabling secure visitor verification across multiple floors.

4. Gate Automation

Dormer auto swing gate with integrated pedestrian side gate control for seamless boundary entry.
Design Intent

Privacy-Led Tropical Security

The home’s central courtyard brings light, air, and greenery deep into the interior. Our security design had to support this balance of openness and privacy without interrupting the calm, sheltered character of the residence.

Courtyard Sanctuary

Strengthening the outer boundary to preserve the internal sanctuary, allowing the courtyard-centred architecture to remain open and tranquil.

Inward Privacy

Implementing security that remains discreet within the refined HYLA Architects design, respecting the home’s privacy within a dense landed context.

Unobtrusive Flow

Ensuring that controlled access and monitoring support the home’s natural ventilation and openness, rather than interrupting it.

Our Approach

Unified Technical Coordination

As the security integration partner for this project, Securevision focused on planning the alarm, CCTV, intercom, and gate control as one coordinated system. This approach supports the architectural concept of inward privacy by ensuring all entry and approach points are managed through a unified technical stack.

By coordinating these systems early, we avoided the technical friction of standalone installations, making visitor access and vehicle entry practical for the family's daily use while maintaining the home’s aesthetic integrity.

Project Priorities

Technical Strategy

  • Coordinate boundary entry with visitor communication
  • Eliminate blind spots across the entire property
  • Maintain architectural minimalist equipment placement
  • Deliver intuitive monitoring across basement to attic
Security Layers

A Layered Residential Stack

The detached house is protected by four technical layers, established to secure the boundary while preserving the openness within.

Intrusion Protection

RISCO LightSYS alarm with 14 zones, providing specific detection points across the perimeter and courtyard access areas.

Surveillance

7-camera Hikvision network providing clear situational awareness of approached points from the street to the side gate.

Visitor Communication

Aiphone JOS intercom with 3 indoor monitors, ensuring clear verification is available on every primary living level.

Entry Control

Dormer auto swing gate and pedestrian side gate control, establishing a secure driveway and foot entry envelope.

Outcome

Controlled Boundary. Preserved Calm.

The final implementation at Siglap Bank delivered layered protection that supports the inward-facing nature of the courtyard home. Residents benefit from clear visitor verification and discreet monitoring across all key approach points.

By establishing strong boundary control at the entry, the system allows the internal courtyard and living spaces to remain open and connected to light and air, secure in the knowledge that the property perimeter is actively protected.

Expert Insight

Architecture-Led Security

For inward-facing landed homes, security should strengthen the boundary while preserving the calm and openness inside.
