About AJAX Systems

Europe's Hub of Wireless Security Innovation

AJAX Systems was founded in 2011 in Kyiv, Ukraine, and has become one of the fastest-growing alarm manufacturers in Europe, with distribution across 130+ countries. The company builds a fully wireless security ecosystem — hub, motion detectors, door contacts, sirens, keypads, and smoke detectors — all communicating via their proprietary Jeweller radio protocol rather than WiFi or standard 433MHz. All system management, including arming, disarming, event history, and live status, is handled through the AJAX app.

AJAX has gained significant traction in Singapore's commercial and high-end residential sectors since 2019, primarily because it solves the installation problem that makes traditional wired alarms impractical in tenanted spaces, conservation shophouses, and completed interiors. Their hub connects to central monitoring via both IP and GSM simultaneously — dual-path communication that meets the redundancy requirements of Singapore's commercial insurance underwriters. Operating temperature range covers Singapore's ambient conditions without any hardware modification.

Why We Carry AJAX

Wireless Without Compromise

Securevision specifies AJAX Systems for projects requiring professional-grade security without the disruption of conduit and wiring.

📡

Jeweller Protocol Eliminates False Alarms

AJAX's Jeweller radio protocol operates on a frequency-hopping spread spectrum that is immune to jamming attempts and interference from WiFi routers. Each detector communicates with the hub every 12 seconds — providing supervision levels that legacy 433MHz systems cannot match.

📸

MotionCam Ends False Alarm Disputes

AJAX's MotionCam captures a photo burst when triggered, sending images to both the monitoring centre and the owner's app. In Singapore, photo verification before dispatch eliminates false alarm fines from the SPF, providing a verified intrusion signal within seconds.

🛠️

Zero Disruption to Completed Interiors

A complete 8-zone commercial system can be installed in a day with no drilling beyond surface-mount screws. This makes AJAX the primary professional option for Singapore's tenanted retail and F&B shopfronts where landlords prohibit extensive conduit work.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

AJAX hubs connect to central monitoring via IP and GSM dual-path — when an intrusion zone triggers, the monitoring centre receives the alert and the corresponding CCTV timestamp is bookmarked automatically, allowing for immediate verified video review.
