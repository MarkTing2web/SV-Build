About GE Caddx

GE Caddx — A Legacy of Alarm Engineering

GE Caddx was a security products brand under GE Security, a division of General Electric, producing wired and hybrid alarm panels under the Networx product name. The NX series panels — particularly the NX-4, NX-6, and NX-8 — were among the most widely installed alarm controllers in Singapore's commercial and residential market through the 1990s and 2000s. GE Security's alarm division was subsequently acquired by UTC Fire & Security and later integrated into the Aritech brand under Carrier Global. The GE Caddx brand name is no longer actively marketed for new product development, and the NX series panels are no longer manufactured.

Despite the brand's discontinuation, GE Caddx Networx panels remain in active service across a significant number of Singapore properties — the hardware was well-built and many installations from the early 2000s continue to function correctly. Local spare parts availability is limited but not exhausted, and Securevision maintains the technical knowledge and component stock to service existing Networx installations. For properties where the panel is still functional and zone coverage is adequate, there is no operational urgency to replace a working system.

Securevision Scope

Securevision maintains existing GE Caddx alarm installations for current clients. GE Caddx panels are no longer specified for new projects — for new alarm installations or full system replacements, we recommend RISCO or AJAX, which offer modern wireless architecture, superior app control, and central monitoring integration.

Why Clients Still Use GE Caddx

Long-Term Alarm System Reliability

While no longer recommended for new projects, working GE Caddx systems continue to provide reliable security for properties that do not require modern app-based control.

🦾

NX Series Panels Were Built to Last

GE's engineering standards produced hardware with low component failure rates — capacitors and transformers specified above the minimum, and PCB traces that have proven corrosion-resistant in Singapore's humidity.

🔌

Wiring Infrastructure Is Retained

Replacing an alarm panel does not require replacing the zone wiring — the PIR detectors and cabling can be re-used with a new panel. For properties where only the panel has failed, the replacement decision is straightforward.

🛑

No Disruption While System Remains Functional

Property managers reasonably defer upgrades when the current system is functioning. A working GE Caddx installation continues to provide intrusion detection and siren output — the core alarm functions — reliably.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

GE Caddx Networx panels report to central monitoring via PSTN or IP communicator add-on. For properties planning migration to RISCO or AJAX, Securevision can manage the full replacement — retaining compatible zone wiring where possible.
