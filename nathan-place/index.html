New Intercom, Access & Vehicle Entry System

Securevision has been appointed to upgrade Nathan Place’s intercom, access and vehicle entry systems for safer, more convenient living.

What’s Changing?

Three key upgrades for a safer and more convenient Nathan Place.

1. Face Recognition & Smart Intercom for Residents and Visitor Access

New Akuvox X915 smart intercoms in the lobbies and main entrances, and Akuvox A05C readers at the gates and gym, using facial recognition for residents and visitor access.

2. Mobile App for Remote Opening

Use the SmartPlus app on your mobile phone to receive visitor calls and open doors remotely.

3. Licence Plate Recognition for Vehicle Entry

Registered resident vehicles will be automatically recognised for entry and exit.

The New Equipment at Nathan Place

A total of 16 pedestrian access points and an upgraded vehicle entry system.

Akuvox X915
Smart Intercom

11 units
  • Installed at Lobby 1 (3), Lobby 2 (3), Lobby 3 (3) and Main Gate (2)
  • For residents and visitors
  • Facial recognition and SmartPlus mobile access for residents
  • Visitors can call residents via intercom

Akuvox A05C
Biometric Reader

5 units
  • Installed at 4 side gates and gym room
  • For resident access only
  • Facial recognition for entry

Licence Plate Recognition (LPR)

  • Cameras recognise registered resident vehicles
  • Automatic entry and exit for a smoother experience
  • Integrated with the condominium’s vehicle access system
