How It Works

A closer look at the new equipment and app going into Nathan Place, and how each part works day to day.

Akuvox X915 Smart Intercom

11 units · Lobbies 1–3 and Main Gate

The Akuvox X915 is a smart door intercom with an 8-inch touchscreen. It supports face recognition, RFID cards, Bluetooth, QR codes and PINs, so entry is quick for residents and simple for visitors. It has a vandal-resistant build and an IP65 weatherproof rating for outdoor use.

  • Residents enter with facial recognition or the SmartPlus mobile app
  • Visitors call residents directly from the intercom
  • Installed at Lobby 1 (3), Lobby 2 (3), Lobby 3 (3) and the Main Gate (2)

Akuvox A05C Biometric Reader

5 units · Side gates and gym room

The Akuvox A05C is a facial recognition reader for resident-only entrances. Registered residents are recognised by their face, so there is no need to touch anything or carry a card.

  • For resident access only
  • Installed at the 4 side gates and the gym room
  • Facial recognition for entry

SmartPlus Mobile App

Your phone as your key

SmartPlus is the mobile app that links your phone to the new intercom system. Once your account is activated, you can receive visitor calls and open doors remotely, wherever you are.

  • Register your email by 18 October 2026 so your account can be set up
  • Accounts are activated on 20 October 2026, after which you can download the app
  • Each household may register up to 4 mobile app users

GantryGo Licence Plate Recognition

Vehicle entry · from 26 Oct 2026

GantryGo licence plate recognition (LPR) cameras read the plates of registered resident vehicles, so the barrier can open automatically as you arrive and leave.

  • Cameras recognise registered resident vehicles
  • Automatic entry and exit for a smoother experience
  • Integrated with the condominium’s vehicle access system

Ready to register?

Your details are needed to activate your SmartPlus account. Registration closes 18 October 2026.
