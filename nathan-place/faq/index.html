Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about the new intercom, access and vehicle entry system at Nathan Place.

Why Upgrade?

Why is the intercom and access system being upgraded?

The existing system is obsolete, and spare parts are no longer available. Replacing it with a new system that uses IP and mobile phone apps ensures reliability and support for the years ahead.

Will the upgrade help to reduce running costs?

It can. The new system allows access to be managed with far less reliance on manual checks. Once the new system is running, this gives the estate room to review its security operating costs, including how the estate is manned.

Registration and the SmartPlus app

Why do I need to register my email?

Your details are used to set up and activate your SmartPlus account. Your account is created using the email address you register, so please use an address you check regularly.

Who should register?

Each household may register up to 4 mobile App Users, including the Master User, using the form on the Nathan Place page. Each App User needs their own name, email address and contact number. You can register everyone together, or each person can register separately. The form shows which App User places are already taken for your unit.

What is a Master User?

Each household can have up to 4 App Users, including the Master User. The person registered as App User 1 is the Master User.

The Master User has administrative rights over the other 3 App Users in the household. From their own SmartPlus app, the Master User can add, change or remove them, and can give a place to a different person.

The Master User should be the Owner, Main Occupant or Main Tenant of the unit. Please choose App User 1 carefully. It should be the person who will manage the household’s app.

If the Master User has not registered yet, you can register as App User 2, 3 or 4 first. Once App User 1 has been registered for a unit, that place is locked and cannot be registered again.

What if a family member is not tech-savvy or does not have a mobile phone? Is there another option?

Yes. We can provide an optional indoor monitor, the Akuvox S562W. Residents can purchase it directly at S$580 before GST, excluding installation. Installation is usually about S$150 to S$300 before GST. All cabling is surface-run with trunking, with no hacking of walls, and we do not do touching up or making good. Only the Master User (App User 1) can request the monitor.

The monitor needs a power supply and uses your home internet connection. See the monitor and a short video.

What is the registration deadline?

Please register by 18 October 2026. After the Managing Agent verifies the registration details, SmartPlus accounts are activated on 20 October 2026.

When can I download the app?

From 20 October 2026, once your account has been activated. SmartPlus is available from the Apple App Store and Google Play.

What do I need to use the app?

A smartphone and an internet connection, either Wi-Fi or mobile data. The app needs the internet to receive visitor calls and open doors remotely.

What can I do with the SmartPlus app?

You can receive visitor calls from the intercom and open doors remotely from your phone. The How It Works page has a short video showing how to use it.

What if I need to change my email or add a user later?

If you want to change your own email as the Master User, please write to the Managing Agent with the following information:

  • Your name
  • Your contact number
  • The email you want to use
  • Your block and unit number

If you want to add a user, or change another App User’s email, the Master User can do it in their own SmartPlus app. To give a place to someone else, change the email address on that App User’s account. Please do not delete the account.

Tenants, moving in and moving out

What if my unit is rented out to tenants?

Who is the Master User is for the owner and the tenant to decide and agree between them. The Master User should be the Owner, Main Occupant or Main Tenant of the unit.

Any change of tenants, whether they move in or move out, should be informed to the Managing Agent.

If the owner is not the Master User and the tenants later move out, the owner can approach the Managing Agent, who can add the owner back as the Master User. This is done on the backend, and the Managing Agent has the Property Management rights to make the change.

What happens when someone moves out, or someone new moves in?

The Master User can change who an App User is, from their own SmartPlus app. Please change the email address on the account, and do not delete the account. When the email on an account is changed, the previous person’s face credentials are cleared. A household can have up to 4 App Users, including the Master User. Please inform the Managing Agent of any change of tenants.

What happens to the face credentials of someone who has moved out?

When a tenant fully moves out and their app account is removed, or the email on the account is changed, their face credentials are cleared. For residents who do not use the app, the Managing Agent removes their face credentials separately.

What happens when a unit has a new owner, or a former owner leaves?

The Managing Agent handles these cases, including setting up the new owner and phasing out the former owner.

Installation and equipment

What is being replaced?

The old intercoms and readers are being replaced with 11 Akuvox X915 smart intercoms and 5 Akuvox A05C biometric readers, a total of 16 pedestrian access points. The vehicle entry system is also upgraded with licence plate recognition.

When will the work be done?

The old intercoms and readers are removed and the new ones installed progressively from 21 to 26 October 2026.

Where will the new equipment be installed?

The X915 intercoms go at Lobby 1 (3), Lobby 2 (3), Lobby 3 (3) and the Main Gate (2). The A05C readers go at the 4 side gates and the gym room.

Access for Residents and Visitors

How do residents enter the building?

Residents can use facial recognition to enter the buildings and also to access the gym room.

How do visitors enter the building?

Visitors can enter the building only by using the intercom to call the resident. At the VCP (visitor call panel), they enter the unit number and speak to the resident. If the resident permits entry, they can remotely open the door using the SmartPlus smartphone app.

How do I enrol my face for facial recognition?

Residents who use the SmartPlus app can enrol their face through the app once their account is activated.

If you do not have the SmartPlus app, please send a clear photo of your face, together with your name, contact number and address, to the Managing Agent at thenathanplace@gmail.com. The Managing Agent will collect and verify the details before sending them to Securevision for enrolment.

Vehicle entry

How does licence plate recognition work?

Licence plate recognition cameras are installed at the gates. They recognise the licence plates of registered vehicles, and once a vehicle is registered and authorised, the gate opens automatically.

Do I need to register my vehicle separately for this?

No separate registration is required.

When does licence plate recognition start?

On 26 October 2026.

How do visitors’ vehicles enter?

There is a visitor call panel at the gate. The visitor can call the resident through the intercom, and the resident can open the gate remotely using their SmartPlus app.

Alternatively, if the visitor knows the resident’s phone number, they can call the resident directly, and the resident can open the gate using the SmartPlus app.

Your personal data

How is my personal data used?

By registering for the SmartPlus app, you consent to your personal data, including that of your users, being shared with Securevision and the Akuvox Cloud for the purposes of registration and use of the SmartPlus app. It is not used for any other purpose.

Does the system store my face or photo?

No. During enrolment, the system uses an algorithm to measure points on the face and creates an encrypted string of numbers. Only this string of numbers is stored, in the intercom and in the cloud. The system does not store your face or any picture of it.

What happens to the photo I send to the Managing Agent?

The Managing Agent verifies your details and sends the photo to Securevision, who enrol your face in the system. After enrolment, the photograph is deleted. Only the encrypted string of numbers is kept in the system.
