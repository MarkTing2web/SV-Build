Basis for zone distances. Zone boundaries use the DORI (Detection, Observation, Recognition, Identification) pixel density standard: detection ≥25 px/m, recognition ≥125 px/m, identification ≥250 px/m. Distances are calculated from the camera’s horizontal resolution and lens field of view. Camera specifications: monitoring (2MP, 4mm, 84° HFOV), recognition (4MP, 4mm, 84° HFOV), identification (4MP, 6mm, 55° HFOV). Actual coverage varies with scene lighting, camera mounting height, and manufacturer firmware. For a certified site assessment, contact Securevision.
