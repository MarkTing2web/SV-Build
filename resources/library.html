Our Selection Criteria

Why These Products Are In Our Library

Not every product reaches this library. The brands and models listed here are ones we have personally selected, installed, commissioned, and supported in Singapore projects across residential, commercial, condominium, industrial, and institutional environments.

Our selection criteria are the same ones we apply when specifying any project: reliability in Singapore's climate, availability of local technical support and spare parts, integration capability with the other systems we deploy, and suitability for the specific property types and use cases we serve. The most expensive product is not always the right choice. The right choice is the one that fits the operational requirement, the maintenance capability of the facilities team, and the budget of the project.

Some products in this library are legacy models we no longer actively specify for new projects: but which many Singapore properties still operate. We maintain documentation for them because our customers need support materials for existing systems, not just the newest hardware.

Common Questions

About This Library

1

What is included in the Product Library?

Datasheets, installation manuals, brochures, product videos, and key technical specifications for the brands and models Securevision actively installs and supports in Singapore. Each category page lists all available assets for every product in that system group.

2

Are these products available in Singapore?

Yes. Products listed are either currently available through Securevision for new installations or commonly encountered in existing Singapore installations we service and maintain. Legacy products are clearly indicated where they are no longer specified for new projects.

3

Can I buy products directly from Securevision?

Some products are available as supply-only items for customers who manage their own installation. Most are typically supplied as part of a complete installation project. Contact us to discuss whether supply-only is appropriate for your specific requirement.

4

Are legacy products included?

Yes. We maintain documentation for older systems because many Singapore properties still operate them. If you need a datasheet, manual, or wiring diagram for a system installed several years ago, this library is the most reliable place to find it without contacting the original manufacturer.

5

I need to understand how a system works, not just its specifications. Where should I go?

The Technical Guides explain how each system type works, what the key specification decisions are, and what common mistakes to avoid: written for non-technical readers. The Brands section explains why we choose specific manufacturers for specific applications.
