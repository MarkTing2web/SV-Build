Planning Calculators — Size Your System Before You Budget

Four interactive tools that answer the most common sizing and budgeting questions before a security installation. Calculate how much NVR storage your cameras will need and how long recordings will last. Find out how many cameras cover your site and what resolution each position needs. Get indicative cost estimates for a CCTV system or a door access control system — broken down by supply, installation, and programming. All figures are Singapore-calibrated. They are indicative, not quoted — a site survey gives you the accurate number, but these tools help you understand scale and prepare a realistic budget before that conversation.

Best for: Operations managers and property owners preparing a capital expenditure request. Anyone who wants to understand rough cost before committing time to a site survey. Budget committee members who need a ballpark figure before approving a procurement process.
