VESTA Smart Living Platform

VESTA is Securevision's smart living platform developed specifically for Singapore condominiums and managed residential communities. It brings together visitor management, smart intercom, vehicle access, facility booking and estate communications within a single resident experience. Instead of managing multiple applications for different systems, residents and management teams can access essential estate services through one platform. Designed around the operational needs of Singapore developments, VESTA helps simplify administration while improving convenience for residents.

HikCentral Professional

HikCentral Professional is a unified security management platform that allows multiple security systems to be monitored and managed from a single interface. CCTV, access control, alarms, intercoms and vehicle management systems can all be brought together into one platform, making it easier for operators and security teams to oversee daily operations. It is commonly deployed in commercial buildings, industrial facilities, institutions and multi-site environments where centralised management is important.

ZKBio CVSecurity

ZKBio CVSecurity is ZKTeco's integrated security management platform designed around access control and identity management. It combines access control, CCTV, visitor management, licence plate recognition, elevator control and attendance functions within a single system. Organisations that standardise on ZKTeco hardware often use ZKBio CVSecurity as the central platform for administration, monitoring and reporting across multiple security functions.
