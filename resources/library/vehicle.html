GANTRYGO · SECUREVISION

GantryGo LPR Platform

GantryGo is Securevision's vehicle management platform designed for condominiums, commercial buildings and managed developments in Singapore. Using licence plate recognition technology, it automates vehicle entry and exit while reducing the need for physical access cards, tags or remote controls. Residents, visitors, delivery vehicles and service providers can be managed through a single platform, helping estates improve both security and convenience. The system integrates with selected barrier brands and provides a complete vehicle movement history for management and security teams.

EBELCO

EM Lock Range (600–2400KGF)

Ebelco electromagnetic locks are commonly used in access control systems for offices, commercial buildings, condominiums and industrial facilities. Available in a range of holding forces, they can be matched to different door types and security requirements. Their fail-safe operation (meaning they unlock automatically when power is lost) makes them suitable for doors that must automatically release during a power failure or emergency condition, and they are commonly integrated with access control systems in commercial and residential applications.

VIRO

Electric Strike

VIRO electric strikes are widely used for pedestrian gates, commercial doors and controlled access points where secure locking is required. Their fail-secure design (meaning they stay locked when power is lost) keeps the door locked during a power failure while still allowing authorised access through a mechanical key when necessary. This makes them particularly suitable for applications where maintaining security during power interruptions is an important consideration.
