How It Works

A Better Way to Assess Your Security Setup

These are not forms that promise to email you a PDF. They are interactive assessments you complete now and get results from immediately.

1

Pick Your Property Type

Choose the checklist that matches your property — commercial, condominium, healthcare, dormitory, or a specific system like intercoms.

2

Work Through the Questions

Tick what you have in place. Each question has a short explanation of why it matters and what the risk is if it is missing.

3

See Your Gap Score

Each section scores as you go. Your overall score appears at the end with a plain-language description of what it means for your property.

4

Act on the Results

Book a site assessment to turn your gap analysis into a written proposal — or WhatsApp us to discuss your results directly.
