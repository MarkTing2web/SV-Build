Foundation of Access Control

1. What Is an Auto Gate System?

An automatic gate system is a motorised assembly that opens and closes a gate using an electric motor, triggered by a remote control, access reader, or sensor.

Auto gate opening at Singapore landed property

More Than Just Convenience

A gate motor that works quietly and reliably every time is easy to take for granted — until it fails. This guide is written for the moment you need to make a real decision: choosing a system for a new home, replacing a failed motor, or upgrading to one with access control integration.

An auto gate system has three core jobs. First, it controls who and what enters your property. Second, it signals to the outside world that the premises are managed and monitored. Third, when integrated with an intercom or access reader, it becomes a fully automated entry management point.

The Gate Motor

The motor generates the torque required to push or pull the gate. Selecting an underpowered motor for your gate is the most common cause of premature failure.

Understanding Your Options

2. Gate Types: Swing vs Sliding

The two dominant types in Singapore — swing and sliding — suit different property configurations and space requirements.

Dual-leaf swing gate opening inward at Singapore terrace house

Swing Gates

A swing gate operates like a door, rotating around a vertical hinge post. As a general rule, gates should swing inward into the premises. Outward swinging is only acceptable when there is no pedestrian risk.

Sliding gate on ground track at Singapore property

Sliding Gates

A sliding gate travels horizontally along a ground track parallel to the boundary wall. It is the preferred solution for properties with limited driveway depth.

Choosing the Right Motor

3. Motor Selection Guide

Concealed Swing Motors

For leaves up to 2m, a concealed motor (like MAG SW200P) is buried within the gate post or ground housing, leaving no visible mechanical components.

Swing Arm Motors

When the gate leaf exceeds 2m, a swing arm motor (like FAAC 415) is the correct choice, often specified for condominium main entrance gates.

Sliding Gate Motors

We carry two FAAC sliding motor models: FAAC 746 (up to 600kg) and FAAC 844 (up to 1,500kg) for industrial premises.

Protecting People and Property

6. Safety Devices

Photocell Safety Beams

A transmitter and receiver mounted on opposite sides of the opening. When interrupted, it signals the control board to halt or reverse the gate.

💡 Securevision Requirement

A gate motor without safety devices is a liability. Photocells and safety edges are standard engineering requirements for any responsibly installed system.

Getting the Specification Right

7. Site Assessment & Planning

Every gate installation begins with a site assessment. Gate width, ground conditions, wall structure, power supply routing, and drainage determine which system is viable.

Gate Opening Width

Measure the clear opening between gate posts. Minimum 3m clear opening for single-lane vehicle access is recommended.

Pillar and Post Strength

The gate motor transfers significant dynamic forces to the gate post. Brick pillars without steel reinforcement can crack over time.

When Something Goes Wrong

11. Common Faults & Troubleshooting

Gate Reverses Immediately

Almost always a safety device trigger. Check for obstructions (leaves, webs) in the photocell path.

Remote Not Working

Try the second remote. If neither works, check the motor's circuit breaker and isolator switch.

Q: How long does a gate motor last?

A well-maintained residential motor should last 10 to 15 years in Singapore's tropical climate.

Q: Can my gate be operated during a power failure?

Yes, if the installation includes a battery backup unit (included in all Securevision installations).
