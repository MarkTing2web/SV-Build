⚠️

These are indicative estimates, not quotations. Actual costs and requirements vary based on site conditions, cable run distances, building structure, existing infrastructure, and specific product selection. A site survey by a Securevision engineer gives you the accurate figures — and it is free and without obligation. Use these calculators to prepare a budget range and understand scale before that conversation.

When to Use These Calculators

Before You Request Proposals

The most useful time to run these calculators is before you speak to any contractor — so you arrive at that conversation with a realistic sense of scale, storage requirements, and budget range. That makes the site survey more productive and helps you evaluate proposals with context.

📋

Budget Planning

Use the cost calculators to prepare a capital expenditure request or board paper with a realistic budget range before committing to a site survey process. The figures are indicative but sufficient for budget approval in most Singapore organisations.

🏗️

Design & Specification

Use the storage and coverage calculators during building design to determine NVR room space requirements, network switch specifications, and cable infrastructure sizing — before M&E drawings are finalised.

✅

Proposal Evaluation

Run the cost calculators after receiving proposals to sense-check whether the figures are within a reasonable range for Singapore market rates. A proposal significantly outside the calculator range — in either direction — warrants clarification.
