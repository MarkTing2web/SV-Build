How these figures are estimated. Bandwidth is derived from typical reference bitrates for each resolution, adjusted for codec (H.265 ≈ half of H.264; H.265+ lower again), frame rate, and scene complexity. Storage uses the standard rate of 1 Mbps of continuous recording ≈ 0.45 GB per hour. Capacities use decimal units (1 TB = 1,000 GB) to match how drives are sold. These are planning estimates only — real figures vary with scene activity, camera firmware, bitrate mode (CBR/VBR), and recorder configuration. For a guaranteed specification, request a proposal.
