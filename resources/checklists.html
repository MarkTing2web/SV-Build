How It Works

What a Checklist Gives You

1

Answer the questions

Work through 26–31 questions about your current security setup. Questions are straightforward — no technical knowledge required. Takes 10–15 minutes.

2

Get your score

Each answer is scored. At the end you receive an overall security posture rating and a breakdown by category — perimeter, access, surveillance, alarm, and compliance.

3

See prioritised gaps

The checklist identifies your highest-priority gaps and explains why each matters. No login required, no data stored. Use it to prepare for a site assessment or an AGM proposal.
