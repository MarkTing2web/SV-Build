Institutional Security Assessment

For schools, religious organisations, government-linked premises, and community institutions. How It Works

What You Get at the End

1

Answer the questions honestly

Work through 26–31 questions about your current setup. No technical knowledge required — the questions are written for property owners and managers, not security engineers. Takes 10–15 minutes.

2

See your score by category

Each answer is weighted. At the end you receive an overall security posture score and a breakdown by category — perimeter, access control, surveillance, alarm, and compliance — so you can see exactly where the weakest areas are.

3

Know what to fix first

The checklist identifies your highest-priority gaps in order of risk, and explains why each one matters. No login required, no data stored, no sales call triggered. Use it to prepare for a site assessment, an AGM proposal, or a conversation with your existing contractor.

Common Questions

About These Checklists

1

What is a security planning checklist?

A structured gap assessment that evaluates your current security setup against best practice for your property type. Each answer is scored and the result shows your overall security posture and highest-priority gaps — without requiring a site visit or a sales conversation.

2

Who should use these?

Property owners, facility managers, MCST council members, and managing agents who want to understand their security gaps before engaging a contractor or preparing a budget proposal. The checklists are designed to be completed without technical knowledge.

3

Is my data stored?

No. The checklists run entirely in your browser. Nothing is submitted, stored, or shared. You can close the page at any time without leaving any record.

4

What happens after I complete one?

You see your score and prioritised gap list immediately. If the results are useful and you want a professional assessment of your specific site, you can request one — but there is no obligation and no automatic follow-up.
