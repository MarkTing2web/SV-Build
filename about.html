Founder's Story

Why Ler Wee Meng Started Securevision

Most security companies in Singapore sell equipment. Securevision was built to do something different — to design systems around how properties actually operate, and to remain accountable long after the installation is completed.

After years in the security industry, Ler Wee Meng saw the same problem repeated across many sites: systems were being installed, but they were not always designed around the client's actual risks, operations, or long-term needs.

Over the course of his career, he has worked across engineering, project management, alarm monitoring, sales leadership, business development, and executive management — in roles spanning residential, commercial, and industrial security environments, as well as large-scale monitored alarm operations serving tens of thousands of customers across Singapore.

Having seen security systems from both technical and operational perspectives, he believes effective security is not about individual products. It is about understanding how systems are designed, used, maintained, and managed over time. That perspective continues to guide Securevision today.

He saw alarms that triggered without clear visibility, camera systems that recorded incidents but did little to prevent them, and installations that looked complete on paper but were weak in real-world use.

That frustration led to the founding of Securevision in 2006.

From the start, the company took a different approach: assess the site carefully, understand how the property actually operates, recommend the right combination of systems, and remain accountable long after handover.

Today, Securevision serves clients across Singapore — landed homes, condominiums, MCSTs and managing agents, commercial offices, factories, warehouses, healthcare facilities, dormitories, and institutions. The company is one of the few Singapore security systems integrators that handles all security system types under one roof, with PLRD licensing, bizSAFE certification, and BCA registration.

Who Contacts Us

Why Clients Engage Securevision

Most clients do not contact us because they need a camera, an alarm, or an access card. They contact us because they have a problem that needs solving.

Renovation & New Build

They are planning a Singapore landed home, condominium, or commercial renovation and want security systems installed properly before the ceilings are closed and the walls are plastered. The window to do it right is narrow and expensive to miss.

Ageing Systems

Their existing intercom, access control, or CCTV system is reaching end of life — spare parts are becoming unavailable, service calls are increasing, or the system no longer meets the operational requirements of the property. They need a clear recommendation: repair, upgrade, or replace.

Condominium & Estate Management

MCSTs and managing agents in Singapore need to upgrade common area systems — intercom, car park access, visitor management, or perimeter CCTV — and want a contractor experienced in the BMSMA approval process and the practical realities of managing works in an occupied development.

🔗

Integration & Simplification

They are managing multiple security systems across an office, factory, warehouse, or commercial building that were installed at different times by different contractors. Systems do not work together, management is fragmented, and they want a single integrated solution with one point of accountability.

Condominiums, commercial buildings, and industrial facilities want to automate car park access — replacing manual card systems with LPR licence plate recognition, reducing guardhouse workload, and improving visitor management. Most are also asking about the ERP 2.0 OBU transition.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions About Securevision

How long has Securevision been operating?

Securevision was founded in 2006 and has been serving residential, commercial, condominium, and industrial clients in Singapore for nearly two decades. The company is owner-operated by its founder Ler Wee Meng, who remains directly involved in client engagements.

Is Securevision PLRD-licensed?

Yes. Securevision holds a current PLRD licence under the Private Security Industry Act — the mandatory requirement for companies installing security systems in Singapore. The licence number is and can be verified at police.gov.sg. Always ask any security contractor for their licence number before engagement.

What types of Singapore properties does Securevision support?

We support Singapore landed homes, condominiums, HDB properties, commercial offices, retail premises, factories, warehouses, healthcare and care facilities, schools, worker dormitories, and public-facing commercial buildings. Each property type has different system requirements and compliance obligations — our approach is calibrated accordingly.

Does Securevision work with MCSTs and Managing Agents?

Yes. A significant portion of our work involves condominium MCSTs and managing agents — covering intercom upgrades, access control, car park LPR, CCTV, and integrated visitor management. We are experienced in the BMSMA approval process and in coordinating works in occupied developments.

What security systems does Securevision install?

We install CCTV and surveillance systems, door access control, video intercom, burglar alarms, auto gates, car park barriers, licence plate recognition, IP telephony, and the network infrastructure that supports all of these. We also develop and deploy our own platforms — VESTA for condominium management and GantryGo for car park LPR.

Does Securevision provide maintenance after installation?

Yes. We provide maintenance, servicing, troubleshooting, upgrades, and lifecycle support for all installed systems. Annual maintenance contracts and time-and-materials support are both available. We do not disappear after the handover.
