How It Works

A closer look at the new equipment and app going into Suites 123, and how each part works day to day.

Akuvox S535 Smart Intercom

2 units · Lobby and lift area, Level 1

The Akuvox S535 is a smart audio-video intercom with an aluminium panel and a 5-inch touchscreen. It supports face recognition, RFID cards, PINs, Bluetooth and QR codes, so entry is quick for residents and simple for visitors. Its wide-angle camera gives a clear view of the person at the door, and it is built for outdoor use.

  • Residents enter with facial recognition or the SmartPlus mobile app
  • Visitors call residents directly from the intercom
  • Installed at the lobby and lift area on Level 1 (2 units)

Akuvox A05C Biometric Reader

2 units · Staircase and gym

The Akuvox A05C is a facial recognition reader with a 5-inch display. Registered residents are recognised by their face, so there is no need to touch anything or carry a card.

  • Installed at the staircase and the gym (2 units)
  • Facial recognition for entry
  • Weatherproof (IP65)

SmartPlus Mobile App

Your phone as your key

SmartPlus is the mobile app that links your phone to the new intercom system. Once your account is activated, you can receive visitor calls, view live video and open doors remotely, wherever you are.

  • Remote door opening, live video and notifications
  • Door logs, and a temporary key (QR code) for visitors
  • Register your email by 8 December 2026 so your account can be set up
  • Accounts are activated on 10 December 2026, after which you can download the app
  • Each household may register up to 4 mobile app users

Ready to register?

Your details are needed to activate your SmartPlus account. Registration closes 8 December 2026.
