About GantryGo

GantryGo — Built in Singapore, for Singapore Properties

GantryGo is a proprietary platform developed by GantryGo Pte Ltd, a company co-founded by Securevision. It was designed specifically to address the operational reality of managing vehicle access and visitor entry in Singapore's high-density residential estates and commercial developments — environments where barrier control, resident authorisation, and audit trails must all function reliably at scale and around the clock.

The platform integrates licence plate recognition cameras at entry and exit points, a cloud-based management dashboard for estate managers, and a resident mobile app for pre-authorising visitors and receiving entry notifications. Unlike generic LPR systems, GantryGo is engineered as a complete workflow — from the moment a visitor's plate is registered to the moment the barrier opens and the event is logged.

Securevision Scope

Securevision specifies, installs, and commissions the full GantryGo system — hardware cameras, barrier interface, cloud software setup, and resident app onboarding. Post-installation support and software updates are managed directly by the GantryGo team.

Why We Carry GantryGo

One System, End to End

We built GantryGo because no existing product in the market delivered the complete workflow our condominium and managed estate clients needed — from plate recognition at the gate to management reporting in the back office.

📷

LPR at the Gate

Dedicated LPR cameras at entry and exit points read licence plates in real time, day and night, including in rain and low-light conditions. Recognised resident vehicles pass through without manual intervention. Unrecognised plates trigger a guard alert or hold at the barrier, depending on site configuration.

📱

Resident Mobile App

Residents pre-authorise visitors by entering their plate number in the app before they arrive. When the visitor's plate is recognised at the gate, the barrier opens automatically. Residents receive a push notification confirming entry — giving them visibility of who has arrived at their home without requiring guard intervention.

☁️

Cloud Management Dashboard

Estate managers access a real-time dashboard showing all vehicle movements, active visitor authorisations, and barrier events. Reports are exportable for MCST records and security reviews. Configuration changes — adding a resident, blocking a plate, adjusting access hours — take effect immediately without on-site intervention.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

GantryGo connects natively with the VESTA™ platform. A vehicle entry event from GantryGo automatically triggers the corresponding entry camera to capture a timestamped image, which is linked to the plate recognition log — giving security staff a complete visual and data record of every vehicle movement.
