About Dormer

Malaysian Recess Gate Motor — The Compact Replacement Option

Dormer is a Malaysian manufacturer of concealed recess swing gate motors. Like MAG, Dormer products have been installed across Singapore landed homes for many years and have accumulated a meaningful installed base. Both brands produce recess motors for the same application — concealed swing gate automation for residential properties — and both are manufactured in Malaysia with similar pricing and spare parts availability.

The practical difference that determines whether Securevision specifies MAG or Dormer for a given job is often the motor housing footprint. MAG and Dormer recess motors have slightly different physical dimensions. For a replacement installation — where the old motor has failed and needs replacing — the new motor must fit the existing cavity in the pillar or the existing mounting plate. If the existing installation used a smaller-footprint motor, Dormer's more compact housing is the practical choice. For new installations, MAG is typically Securevision's default selection due to its slightly stronger brand presence and support infrastructure, but both are viable and the selection depends on site-specific requirements.

Securevision Scope

Securevision installs and services Dormer recess swing gate motors for Singapore landed homes. Dormer is specified primarily for replacement installations where the existing motor footprint is a constraint, and as an alternative to MAG for new residential recess motor installations. For swing gates wider than 2 metres, Securevision specifies a FAAC 415 surface-mounted arm rather than a recess motor from either brand.

Replacement vs New Installation — How We Choose

🔄

Replacement Installations — Footprint Drives the Decision

When a recess swing gate motor fails and needs replacing, the most practical approach is to fit the new motor into the existing housing cavity without major modification to the pillar or gate structure. This requires matching the footprint of the replacement motor to the existing installation.

Dormer's more compact motor housing makes it the right choice when the existing installation used a smaller-footprint motor — fitting MAG into the same space would require enlarging the cavity or fabricating a new mounting platform, adding cost and installation time. Securevision assesses the existing installation before recommending a replacement brand, specifically to determine which fits the available space without modification.

🏡

New Installations — When Dormer Makes Sense

For new concealed swing gate motor installations at Singapore landed homes where the gate leaf is under 2 metres wide and the weight is within the recess motor range, Dormer is a practical and cost-effective specification. The motor is reliable, the spare parts are available through Malaysian supply chains, and the installation is similar to MAG.

Securevision's selection between MAG and Dormer for new installations is driven by availability, site-specific requirements, and the client's existing gate hardware. Both brands share a similar service life of 3 to 5 years under regular use — gear wear is the common end-of-life failure mode for both, and this is normal and expected for recess motor technology at this price point.
