About Aiphone

The Standard for Integrated Communication Solutions

Aiphone is a world-renowned leader in professional communication and intercom technologies. By specialising in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial systems, Aiphone has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimised for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, Aiphone is the operational standard for established landed properties and high-security enterprise facilities. Their JO series provide the required scalability for residential sites, while their robust IX series delivers professional-grade protection for modern environments.
