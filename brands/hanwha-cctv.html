About Hanwha Vision

The Standard for Enterprise Video Surveillance

Hanwha Vision is a world-renowned leader in professional communication and surveillance technologies. By specialising in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial surveillance systems, Hanwha Vision has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimised for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, Hanwha Vision is the operational standard for established commercial complexes and high-security government facilities. Their Wisenet series provide the required scalability for large-scale sites, while their robust AI engine delivers professional-grade protection for modern environments.
