About Hikvision

The Standard for Integrated Surveillance Solutions

Hikvision is a world-renowned leader in professional communication and surveillance technologies. By specialising in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial surveillance systems, Hikvision has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimised for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, Hikvision is the operational standard for established landed properties and high-security enterprise facilities. Their ColorVu series provide the required scalability for residential sites, while their robust AcuSense engine delivers professional-grade protection for modern environments.
