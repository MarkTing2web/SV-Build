About Milestone Systems

Milestone Systems — Open Platform VMS Since 1998

Milestone Systems is a Danish software company founded in 1998 and now part of the Canon Group. Their flagship product, XProtect, is one of the most widely deployed video management software platforms in the world, with over 500,000 installations across retail, infrastructure, education, healthcare, and critical facilities. XProtect is open-platform by design — it supports more than 8,000 cameras and devices from virtually every major manufacturer, and integrates with over 1,000 third-party applications through published APIs and SDKs.

In Singapore, Milestone XProtect is the preferred VMS platform for medium-to-large commercial and institutional sites that require centralised video management, multi-site visibility, and integration with access control or alarm systems. Its scalable licensing model — from XProtect Express+ for smaller sites to XProtect Corporate for enterprise deployments — makes it appropriate across a wide range of project sizes.

Why We Carry Milestone

The Platform That Works With Every Camera

We specify Milestone XProtect for projects where the client has existing cameras from multiple manufacturers, needs multi-site management, or requires integration between video and other security systems.

🔓

Open Platform — No Camera Lock-In

XProtect integrates with more than 8,000 cameras and devices across every major manufacturer. This means clients are never locked into a single hardware brand. Existing cameras can be retained during a software upgrade, and new cameras from any compatible manufacturer can be added without replacing the platform.

🏢

Multi-Site Centralised Management

Milestone Interconnect allows operators to manage video feeds from multiple sites — including remote properties — from a single interface. For clients managing multiple buildings or a network of facilities, this eliminates the need for separate NVR systems at each location.

🔗

Access Control and Alarm Integration

XProtect integrates with access control systems so badge events can be cross-referenced against video footage in real time. When a door alarm or forced-entry event is triggered, operators can immediately pull up the relevant camera without switching systems, cutting investigation time significantly.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

Milestone XProtect connects to our VESTA™ platform — access control events from Suprema or HID systems can automatically trigger XProtect to begin recording on the relevant camera and bookmark the footage, creating a linked evidence trail across both systems.
