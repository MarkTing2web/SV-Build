About HikCentral Professional

One Platform for Every Hikvision Security System

HikCentral Professional is Hikvision's enterprise security management platform — a client-server software system that manages Hikvision CCTV cameras, access control panels, alarm systems, video intercoms, ANPR cameras, and attendance terminals from a single interface. It is the platform Securevision recommends when a Singapore client needs more than basic NVR viewing — when they need to integrate multiple security systems, manage multiple sites, run video analytics, generate compliance reports, or centralise security operations across a large estate.

HikCentral is modular — clients purchase only the licence modules they need, starting with a base licence and adding camera channels, access control doors, alarm inputs, ANPR channels, and specialist modules as their requirements grow. Licences are perpetual: a one-time purchase with no annual renewal, and software upgrades are provided free of charge. For Singapore businesses making a long-term commitment to a security management platform, the perpetual licensing model is a meaningful advantage over subscription-based competitors.

Why HikCentral Professional

Four Reasons to Unify on HikCentral

🔗

CCTV and Access Control in One Interface

The most practically valuable capability of HikCentral is the integration between CCTV and access control. When an access event occurs — a door opened, an access denied, an anti-passback violation — HikCentral automatically bookmarks the corresponding camera footage at that exact timestamp. Security personnel reviewing an incident do not need to search manually through footage; they navigate to the access event and the video is already cued to the right moment.

The same integration works in reverse: a motion detection event on a camera can trigger an access alert, a door lock, or an alarm output. For Singapore facilities managers who currently run their Hikvision cameras and their access control in completely separate applications — different software, different logins, different audit trails — the consolidation onto HikCentral removes that operational split entirely.

🏢

Multi-Site Management — All Locations from One Console

HikCentral Professional supports multi-site deployments where cameras and access control hardware at multiple locations are all managed from a single central server. The security manager in Singapore's head office sees live feeds and access logs from the Jurong factory, the Tampines warehouse, and the CBD office simultaneously — in one interface, with one login, under one user permission structure.

For Singapore security guard companies managing multiple client sites — each with their own Hikvision CCTV and access control — HikCentral's remote site management module allows a single control room to monitor all client premises. Alarms from any site appear on the central console in real time. The HikCentral V3.0 clustering architecture supports enterprise-scale deployments designed for large retail chains, multi-site banking networks, and smart communities — distributing server load and ensuring high availability.

📊

Modular Licensing — Pay for What You Need

HikCentral's licensing is modular and perpetual. The base Video Surveillance System (VSS) licence covers the platform itself. Camera channels are added per camera — each camera requires one channel licence. Access Control requires an ACS base licence plus per-door licences. Alarm management, ANPR, time attendance, video intercoms, and specialist analytics modules are all separate, addable licences.

This means a small Singapore office starting with 8 cameras and 4 access doors pays for an 8-camera VSS licence and a 4-door ACS licence — not a blanket enterprise subscription. As the business grows and adds cameras or locations, licences are added incrementally. All licences are perpetual with a one-time purchase, and Hikvision provides software upgrades free of charge. There is no annual renewal pressure and no sudden cost escalation if the subscription model changes.

🤖

Video Analytics and AI Search — AcuSeek

HikCentral Professional includes Hikvision's AcuSeek video content analysis — intelligent search across recorded footage using AI-powered detection. Rather than manually scrubbing through hours of recording to find a specific event, an operator can search for "person in red jacket entering through gate B between 2pm and 4pm yesterday" and AcuSeek returns the matching clips.

For Singapore condominiums and commercial properties where incident investigation is a regular requirement — identifying who accessed a particular area, tracking a vehicle through the car park, verifying a delivery time — AcuSeek dramatically reduces the time and effort required. Cross-system search integrates access control events with camera footage, creating a unified timeline that links card swipes, face recognition events, and vehicle LPR with the corresponding video evidence.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

HikCentral Professional integrates natively with Hikvision MinMoe access control terminals, Hikvision video intercoms, Hikvision ANPR cameras, and Hikvision alarm panels — all managed from the same platform that manages cameras. For Securevision deployments combining Hikvision CCTV with Hikvision access control, HikCentral is the management layer that ties them together. The Hik-Connect mobile app provides remote monitoring and access event viewing for smaller deployments; HikCentral Professional is the enterprise-grade alternative for larger or multi-site installations.
