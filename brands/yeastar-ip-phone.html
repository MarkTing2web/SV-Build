About Yeastar

The Standard for Integrated Telephony Solutions

Yeastar is a world-renowned leader in professional communication and telephony technologies. By specializing in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial telephony systems, Yeastar has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimized for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.
