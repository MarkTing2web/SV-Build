About HID Global

The Standard for Secure Identity Solutions

HID Global is a world-renowned leader in professional identity and secure technologies. By specialising in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial identity systems, HID Global has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimised for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, HID Global is the operational standard for established commercial complexes and high-security government facilities. Their iCLASS SE series provide the required scalability for large-scale sites, while their robust Seos engine delivers professional-grade protection for modern environments. Securevision specifies HID Global for projects that require the industry's most robust identity logic combined with professional-grade hardware stability.
