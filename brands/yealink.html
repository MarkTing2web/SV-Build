About Yealink

Yealink — The World's Leading IP Phone Manufacturer

Yealink is a Chinese communications hardware company and the number-one supplier of IP phones by global market share, with products deployed in more than 140 countries. Their SIP phone range — from the entry-level T3 series to the flagship T8 series with touchscreen displays — covers every tier of business communication from basic desk extensions to executive handsets with video support. Yealink phones are certified for use with all major SIP PBX platforms, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and standard SIP servers.

In Singapore, Yealink phones are widely deployed across commercial offices, hotels, schools, and healthcare facilities. Their PoE-powered handsets eliminate the need for separate power adaptors, and their HD audio quality with Smart Noise Filtering makes them particularly effective in busy open-plan environments. Lantone Systems is the exclusive Yealink distributor in Singapore.

Securevision Scope

Securevision supplies and installs Yealink SIP desk phones as the endpoint hardware for office telephone deployments. We pair Yealink phones with Yeastar PBX systems — see our Yeastar page for PBX configuration scope. We do not provide Microsoft Teams-certified Yealink phones or Yealink video conferencing systems as standalone products.

Why We Carry Yealink

Clear Audio, Simple Deployment, SIP Compatibility

We specify Yealink because the phones pair reliably with Yeastar PBX, require minimal per-handset configuration, and deliver audio quality that users notice — particularly in Singapore's open-plan offices and high-ambient-noise environments.

🎙️

Smart Noise Filtering

Yealink's proprietary noise filtering technology removes background noise from both the microphone and speaker path. In Singapore's open-plan offices and trading floors, this materially improves call intelligibility for both the local user and the remote party — reducing the "repeat yourself" friction that degrades call productivity.

🔌

PoE-Powered, Single Cable

Every Yealink T-series phone is powered over the Ethernet cable via 802.3af/at PoE. No power adaptor, no second cable run to the desk. In office fit-outs where cable management is constrained, this simplifies installation and produces a cleaner result. The passthrough Gigabit port means the computer at the same desk shares the same Ethernet drop.

📋

Yealink Device Management Platform

All deployed Yealink phones are managed centrally through the Yealink Device Management Platform (YDMP). Firmware updates, extension assignment, speed dial configuration, and call log retrieval can all be performed remotely without touching the physical handset — reducing the time cost of desk moves, staff changes, and routine maintenance.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

Yealink phones registered to a Yeastar PBX integrate with the intercom layer of our security stack. An incoming call from an Akuvox door station can ring directly to a designated Yealink handset — giving reception staff or security personnel a door-answering workflow through the same desk phone they use for all other calls.
