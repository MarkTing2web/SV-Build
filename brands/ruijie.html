About Ruijie / Reyee

Ruijie Networks — Purpose-Built for Surveillance Infrastructure

Ruijie Networks is a Chinese networking equipment manufacturer with a strong presence across Asia-Pacific. Their Reyee product line targets small and medium businesses with cloud-managed switches, routers, and wireless access points. Ruijie Reyee surveillance switches are purpose-designed for CCTV and IP security networks — they include native IP camera recognition, automatic loop prevention, and intelligent PoE adaptive features that standard office switches do not offer.

In Singapore, Reyee PoE switches are increasingly deployed in security installations where surveillance-specific features — remote PoE port reboot, extended 250-metre PoE transmission, and camera-aware VLAN configuration — offer operational advantages over standard managed switches. The Ruijie Cloud platform allows remote monitoring and management with no ongoing subscription cost.

Securevision Scope

Securevision specifies Ruijie Reyee PoE switches as the network switching layer for IP camera and access control infrastructure. Our scope covers switch selection, rack mounting or DIN rail mounting, VLAN configuration for security network segmentation, and Ruijie Cloud setup for remote monitoring. We do not provide general enterprise Ruijie core routing or campus network projects.

Why We Carry Ruijie Reyee

Switching Designed for Cameras, Not Just Computers

Standard office switches treat all PoE devices identically. Reyee surveillance switches understand the difference — they include native features that directly address the failure modes and operational demands of IP camera networks.

📷

IP Camera Auto-Recognition

Reyee switches automatically identify IP cameras connected to each PoE port. This allows the switch to apply camera-specific optimisations — including extended transmission distance, isolated loop prevention, and automatic PoE recovery — without manual configuration on a per-port basis.

🔄

Remote PoE Port Reboot

When an IP camera locks up or loses its video stream, the standard fix is a physical power cycle. Reyee switches allow our engineers to reboot individual PoE ports remotely through the Ruijie Cloud platform — resolving the majority of camera hang issues without dispatching a technician on site.

📡

250-Metre Extended PoE Transmission

Standard PoE has a 100-metre cable limit. Reyee's extended mode pushes this to 250 metres at reduced speed — practical for large industrial sites, carparks, and perimeter camera runs where pulling additional fibre or placing intermediate switches would add significant cost.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

Reyee switches sit beneath the camera layer of every IP surveillance network we build. Hikvision and Hanwha camera streams, Akuvox intercom signals, and access controller data all pass through the Reyee-managed switching infrastructure. Combined with Ruijie Cloud remote monitoring, our team can detect and resolve network-layer issues before they affect camera uptime.
