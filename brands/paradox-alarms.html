About Paradox

The Standard for Integrated Intrusion Solutions

Paradox is a world-renowned leader in professional intrusion and security technologies. By specialising in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial alarm systems, Paradox has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimised for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, Paradox is the operational standard for established landed properties and high-security enterprise facilities. Their EVO series provide the required scalability for residential sites, while their robust Digiplex engine delivers professional-grade protection for modern environments. Securevision specifies Paradox for projects that require the industry's most robust intrusion logic combined with professional-grade hardware stability.
