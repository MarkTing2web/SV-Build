About Viro

Viro — Italian Security Hardware Since 1900

Viro S.p.A. is an Italian manufacturer of mechanical and electromechanical security hardware, including padlocks, cylinders, electric strikes, and gate locks. Founded in 1900 and headquartered in Italy, Viro produces a specialist range of outdoor electric locks designed for sliding gates, swing gates, and automatic barrier systems. Their hardware is engineered to withstand extreme temperature ranges — a critical property for Singapore's climate — and is widely used across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East for high-security gate applications.

In Singapore, Viro electric locks are specified primarily for residential auto-gate systems and commercial sliding gate installations where the lock must tolerate tropical heat, humidity, and the thermal expansion that affects metal gates across a 30°C daily temperature swing. The V09 sliding gate lock — Viro's flagship gate product — is designed specifically to accommodate up to 9.6mm of thermal expansion, making it the most mechanically reliable option for Singapore's outdoor gate applications.

Securevision Scope

Securevision supplies and installs Viro electric gate locks (V09, V90, V97, Block-Out series) and Viro heavy-duty padlocks for gate and door applications. We integrate Viro locks with auto-gate motors and access control systems. We do not supply Viro cylinder-only products or Viro safe products as standalone items.

Why We Carry Viro

Hardware Built for Outdoor Singapore Conditions

We specify Viro for gate and outdoor door applications because Italian electromechanical engineering tolerates the conditions that commonly cause cheaper locks to fail — tropical heat, humidity, salt air near the coast, and the mechanical stress of high-cycle gate systems.

🌡️

Thermal Expansion Tolerance

Singapore's metal gates can expand and contract by several millimetres across a single day's temperature cycle. Viro's V09 sliding gate lock is the only gate electric lock engineered specifically to accommodate up to 9.6mm of thermal expansion — the equivalent of a 100°C temperature change on an 8-metre gate — while maintaining reliable lock engagement throughout.

🌧️

Galvanised for Outdoor Permanence

Viro outdoor locks are manufactured from galvanised steel with zinc-plated internals, designed for indefinite outdoor exposure. Unlike stainless steel alternatives that pit in Singapore's humid air near coastal properties, galvanised Viro hardware maintains surface integrity over years of outdoor installation.

🔄

Auto-Gate Compatible

Viro electric locks interface directly with standard auto-gate motor control boards — FAAC, MAG, and most other brands we install. The lock integrates into the gate's existing wiring loom, and the manual deadbolt ensures the gate can be secured mechanically after hours even if the motor is unblocked.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

Viro electric gate locks integrate with access controllers and LPR systems in our stack. A vehicle entry triggered by GantryGo LPR or an access card read can release the Viro lock on the pedestrian gate simultaneously — creating a coordinated vehicle and pedestrian access event with a single credential.
