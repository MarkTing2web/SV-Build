About FAAC

The Standard for Integrated Automation Solutions

FAAC is a world-renowned leader in professional communication and automation technologies. By specialising in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial automation systems, FAAC has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimised for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, FAAC is the operational standard for established landed properties and high-security industrial facilities. Their barrier series provide the required scalability for commercial sites, while their robust motor engine delivers professional-grade protection for modern environments. Securevision specifies FAAC for projects that require the industry's most robust automation logic combined with professional-grade hardware stability.
