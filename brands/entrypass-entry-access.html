About EntryPass

A Malaysian Access Control Brand With a Strong Singapore Track Record

EntryPass Corporation was founded in Malaysia in 2002 and awarded MSC (Multimedia Super Corridor) pioneer status by the Malaysian government in 2003. They develop and manufacture networked access control systems — controllers, software, time attendance terminals, elevator control, car park integration, and visitor management — and have established a significant Singapore presence alongside their Malaysian home market. Their N-series networked controllers are widely installed across Singapore commercial buildings, offices, and industrial premises.

EntryPass gained its Singapore foothold during a period when the alternatives were either expensive western brands or early-stage Chinese products that had not yet established a reliability track record. As a regional Malaysian manufacturer — geographically close, with English-language support and a local distribution network — EntryPass offered a practical middle-ground: more affordable than Lenel or Software House, more familiar to Singapore engineers than emerging Chinese brands. Many Singapore buildings installed EntryPass systems during the 2000s and 2010s, and Securevision continues to service and expand a significant number of these installations. For new projects today, we recommend ZKTeco as our primary access control platform — the combination of biometric depth, competitive pricing, and a comprehensive management platform is difficult to match. For existing EntryPass installations, we are fully equipped to service, expand, and support them.

Why Clients Choose EntryPass

What Made EntryPass Popular in Singapore

EntryPass built its Singapore market position on reliability, regional support, and a willingness to integrate with third-party biometric readers — giving installers flexibility that more proprietary systems could not offer.

🔗

Third-Party Reader Integration — Suprema Compatibility

One of EntryPass's practical advantages over more proprietary systems is its openness to third-party readers. EntryPass controllers work with Suprema biometric readers — FaceStation, BioStation, and BioEntry series — allowing the access controller and the biometric reader to be specified independently. A client who wants enterprise-grade Suprema fingerprint accuracy at the door can use an EntryPass controller at the panel. This flexibility is one reason Securevision has specified EntryPass for projects requiring high-quality biometric verification alongside a familiar management platform.

🏢

Complete Building Integration — Elevator, Car Park, and Visitor

EntryPass covers more than door access. Their platform includes elevator access control (restricting which floors a credential can reach), car park barrier integration, guard tour management, and visitor management — all managed from the same Platform 1 software. For Singapore commercial buildings where the brief includes lift access restriction alongside door control, EntryPass has native support for this without requiring a separate elevator access controller. This integrated scope contributed to its adoption across multi-tenanted commercial buildings in Singapore.

✅

Proven Reliability — Long Singapore Installed Base

EntryPass systems installed in Singapore during the 2000s and 2010s continue to operate reliably. Securevision maintains and services a significant number of these installations — panels, door stations, and elevator modules that have been running for ten or more years without hardware replacement. For building owners and facilities managers with long-established EntryPass systems, the practical question is not whether to replace a working system but whether to expand it. Securevision can do both — expand existing EntryPass installations and advise honestly on when a migration to a more current platform makes financial sense.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

EntryPass controllers connect to Hikvision NVR systems for access-linked CCTV bookmarking — an access denied event at an EntryPass door station triggers a timestamped clip on the nearest camera. For buildings where Suprema biometric readers are paired with EntryPass controllers, the biometric verification happens at the Suprema reader and the access decision is relayed to the EntryPass panel — combining Suprema's recognition accuracy with EntryPass's door management infrastructure.
