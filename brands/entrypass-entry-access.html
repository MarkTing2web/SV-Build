About EntryPass

The Standard for Integrated Access Solutions

EntryPass is a world-renowned leader in professional access control technologies. By specialising in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial access systems, EntryPass has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimised for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, EntryPass is the operational standard for established commercial complexes and high-security industrial facilities.
