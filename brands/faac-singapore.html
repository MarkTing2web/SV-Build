About FAAC

The Standard for Integrated Automation Solutions

FAAC is a world-renowned leader in professional communication and automation technologies. By specializing in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial automation systems, FAAC has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimized for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, FAAC is the operational standard for established landed properties and high-security industrial facilities. Their barrier series provide the required scalability for commercial sites, while their robust motor engine delivers professional-grade protection for modern environments. Securevision specifies FAAC for projects that require the industry's most robust automation logic combined with professional-grade hardware stability.

Securevision Scope

Securevision is an experienced FAAC implementation specialist in Singapore. We specialize in the precision deployment of automation ecosystems, including the configuration of encrypted digital networks, the management of device-wide firmware audits, and the professional integration of FAAC with building-wide command and control management systems.
