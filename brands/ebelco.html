About EBELCO

EBELCO — Electromagnetic Lock Specialists Since 1994

Founded in 1994 as BELCOM Alarms & Supplies, EBELCO is a Malaysia-headquartered manufacturer specialising in electromagnetic door locks, door holders, access accessories, and vibration sensors. Their products are UL-listed, CE-certified, and tested in PSB laboratories in Singapore. EBELCO hardware has been installed across more than 50 countries including landmark buildings such as the Petronas Twin Towers in Malaysia and international airports in Singapore, Italy, and the Netherlands.

In Singapore, EBELCO is a widely adopted standard for electromagnetic locking across commercial, industrial, and institutional access control systems. The brand's IP-rated outdoor units and range of bracket configurations — Z-bracket, L-bracket, glass door U-bracket — make it suitable for Singapore's humid tropical environment and the full range of door types encountered across the market.

Securevision Scope

Securevision supplies and installs the EBELCO electromagnetic lock range, including the E600, E800, E1200 and E2400 series EM locks and the full range of associated mounting brackets, door contacts, exit push buttons, and power supplies. We do not supply EBELCO alarm panels or vibration sensors as standalone products.

Why We Carry EBELCO

Reliable Hardware, Zero Moving Parts

We specify EBELCO for access control installations that require a certified, maintenance-free locking mechanism with a proven track record across Singapore's commercial and institutional sectors.

🔒

Certified Holding Force

EBELCO EM locks are UL-listed and CE-certified. Each model is rated to a verified holding force — 600 lbs for standard timber and glass doors up to 2400 lbs for heavy metal gates — giving us a precise specification tool for every door type and risk level we encounter.

🌧️

Fail-Safe by Design

Every EBELCO EM lock releases on power loss, making it compliant with Singapore's emergency egress requirements without additional hardware. Paired with a backup power supply, the lock remains energised for up to 4 hours during an outage — critical for overnight security in unmanned sites.

🏢

Bracket Range for Every Door

EBELCO's bracket system covers inward swing, outward swing, glass doors, and double doors without requiring custom fabrication. Z-brackets, L-brackets, U-brackets, and armature housing brackets for fire doors are all stock items, which reduces installation time and eliminates site delays.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

EBELCO EM locks connect directly to any ONVIF-compatible access controller in our stack. A door-forced or door-held-open alert from the controller can trigger a Hikvision or Hanwha camera to begin recording at the relevant door — linking the lock event to visual evidence in real time.
