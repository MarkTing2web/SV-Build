About DSC

The Standard for Integrated Intrusion Solutions

DSC is a world-renowned leader in professional intrusion and alarm technologies. By specialising in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial alarm systems, DSC has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimised for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, DSC is the operational standard for established landed properties and high-security enterprise facilities. Their PowerSeries series provide the required scalability for residential sites, while their robust PowerG engine delivers professional-grade protection for modern environments. Securevision specifies DSC for projects that require the industry's most robust intrusion logic combined with professional-grade hardware stability.

Securevision is an experienced DSC implementation specialist in Singapore.
