About MicroEngine

The Standard for Integrated Access Solutions

MicroEngine is a world-renowned leader in professional communication and access technologies. By specialising in the engineering of industrial-grade residential and commercial access systems, MicroEngine has introduced a series of technology standards that have defined the modern security industry. Their hardware is optimised for extreme versatility, delivering exceptional reliability in even the most mission-critical environments.

In Singapore, MicroEngine is the operational standard for established commercial complexes and high-security industrial facilities. Their xPortal series provide the required scalability for large-scale sites, while their robust xPortal engine delivers professional-grade protection for modern environments. Securevision specifies MicroEngine for projects that require the industry’s most robust access logic combined with professional-grade hardware stability.
