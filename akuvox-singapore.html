About Akuvox

Akuvox — Leading IP Video Intercom Innovation

Akuvox is a global leader in smart interpersonal communication, specialising in IP-based video intercom systems that bridge the gap between traditional door access and modern smart home technology. Their product range includes Android-powered touchscreen door stations, indoor monitors, and a robust cloud platform (Akuvox Cloud) that enables residents to receive video calls and unlock gates directly from their smartphones. By leveraging standard PoE (Power over Ethernet) and SIP protocols, Akuvox simplifies the complex wiring of multi-tenant condominium intercoms into a scalable network infrastructure.

In the Singapore market, Akuvox has become the primary choice for "intercom-to-app" conversions in existing condominiums and for new-build luxury landed properties. Unlike traditional analogue systems that require extensive riser cabling to every unit, Akuvox's IP architecture allows for faster deployment and integration with existing building IP networks. The brand's focus on open standards ensures that an Akuvox intercom can serve as a unified entry point, combining video communication with card/face access and lift control management.

Why We Carry Akuvox

Smart, Connected Entry Control

Akuvox solves the "missing call" problem of traditional intercoms by routing every visitor interaction directly to the resident's mobile device via the cloud.

📱

Akuvox Cloud Enables Remote Visual Verification

Residents can see and speak to visitors from anywhere via the SmartPlus app. This mobile-first approach is essential for Singapore landed homes where homeowners are often away or in areas of the house where a fixed indoor monitor cannot be heard.

🧩

SIP Open Standards For Easy Integration

Because Akuvox uses standard SIP protocols, it integrates natively with PBX systems, home automation controllers (Control4, Savant), and third-party IP cameras. It is a "future-proof" hardware choice that doesn't lock the building into a proprietary closed loop.

✨

High-Res Touchscreens Enhance Property Value

The industrial design of Akuvox door stations — using aluminium, Gorilla Glass, and high-brightness Android touchscreens — provides a premium interface at the building entry that significantly enhances the perceived value of modern condominium lobbies.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

Akuvox door stations integrate with the VESTA platform — when a visitor calls, the event is logged and the station's built-in 2MP camera serves as a secondary CCTV angle, with the footage recorded on the main site NVR for unified security reporting.
