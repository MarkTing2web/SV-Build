#### Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: The `systems/` pages suffer from structural "AI sameness." A rigid reliance on a single typographic voice (Montserrat) across headings and body copy makes the pages feel monotonous. The copy rhythm is interrupted constantly by em-dashes, serving as a crutch in place of strong structural typography or varied punctuation.

**Deterministic scan**: The CLI scan found 69 design system violations. Key findings include heavy em-dash overuse (e.g., 159 instances on the platform page alone), undocumented fonts loaded via Google Fonts (Montserrat, Inter, Outfit) that aren't in `DESIGN.md`, the "single font" anti-pattern (Montserrat used for everything), and several undocumented hardcoded colors (e.g., `rgba(0,0,0,0.78)`, `rgba(0,0,0,0.45)`). A marketing buzzword ("future-proof") was also detected.

**Visual overlays**: CLI deterministic scan provided comprehensive coverage; no reliable user-visible overlay was injected for the batch scan context.

#### Overall Impression
The underlying architecture of the content is strong, but the presentation is generic and fatigued by poor typographic choices and punctuation crutches. The single biggest opportunity is to enforce a rigorous typographic hierarchy that aligns with `DESIGN.md`.

#### What's Working
- **Content Architecture**: The breakdown into six distinct system groups with a cohesion flow provides a very solid mental model.
- **FAQ Integration**: Embedding the FAQ directly on the page adds strong SEO value and handles common operational objections well.

#### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Typographic Drift and Monotony**
  - **Why it matters**: Loading multiple undocumented fonts (Inter, Outfit) but using only one (Montserrat) for everything creates a generic, low-effort aesthetic and ignores brand standards.
  - **Fix**: Re-align the font stack to match `DESIGN.md` strictly. Use a distinct pairing for headings vs. body copy.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

- **[P2] Em-dash Overuse**
  - **Why it matters**: 100+ em-dashes per page is an undeniable AI generation artifact. It ruins reading cadence and makes the brand sound breathless.
  - **Fix**: Audit and replace em-dashes with strong punctuation (periods, colons) or structural layout breaks.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

- **[P2] Undocumented Colors**
  - **Why it matters**: Hardcoded values like `rgba(0,0,0,0.78)` bypass the CSS variables, causing unpredictable states across components and maintenance overhead.
  - **Fix**: Replace all literal RGBA definitions with existing design system tokens or formalize them in the tonal ramps.
  - **Suggested command**: `/impeccable colorize`

#### Persona Red Flags

**Jordan (First-Timer)**: The over-reliance on em-dashes and lack of clear typographic distinction between concepts might cause cognitive overload when trying to understand how the six systems connect.
