## Anti-Patterns Verdict

Montserrat is loaded as the primary font, but due to body class styling rules, it overrides or dominates body text on key cards (e.g. `.hp-start-card-body`, `.hp-problem-body`), leading to a monotonous appearance. 

The automated scanner detected **4 issues**:
- `overused-font`: Montserrat (line 20)
- `single-font`: Only Montserrat detected for everything (line 20)
- `em-dash-overuse`: 40 instances of em-dashes in body copy (a strong indicator of AI-generated content)
- `numbered-section-markers`: Sequence of display markers 01-06 used sequentially as layout scaffolds

## Overall Impression

The index page is structurally sound and highly informative. It clearly addresses its key sectors, but the copywriting and typography display severe signs of "AI slop" tells—specifically the massive overuse of em-dashes to connect clauses, and the lack of font contrast where Montserrat bleeds into body paragraphs.

## What's Working

- **Clear Sector Routing**: The "Where Do You Fit?" cards segment target audiences (MCSTs, Landed Homeowners, Commercial Managers) brilliantly.
- **Trust Elements**: Banners displaying bizSAFE Level 3, Police Licensing, and BCA registrations immediately establish compliance authority.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] AI Cadence Tells (Em-dash Overuse)
- **Why it matters**: 40 em-dashes across the page copy ("— not installed as separate parts", "— sharing data, triggering each other") make the site feel like it was generated by an AI assistant rather than written by a security expert.
- **Fix**: Remove em-dashes. Rewrite sentences using colons, commas, semicolons, or separate sentences.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`

### [P1] Typographic Monotony
- **Why it matters**: Montserrat is bleeding into body cards and lists, overriding Inter. This creates a thick, monotonous layout that is harder to read for dense technical prose.
- **Fix**: Re-evaluate selectors in `sv-homepage.css` and enforce Inter (`font-family: 'Inter', sans-serif`) on all descriptions, card bodies, and lists.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable typeset`

### [P2] Visual Clutter (Emoji Icons)
- **Why it matters**: The use of generic emojis (🏠, 🏢, 🏬, 🏭, 📚, 🗂️) in the entry point and resources grids undercuts the professional, engineered brand personality.
- **Fix**: Replace emojis with clean SVG icons (or simple typography layouts) that match the Blueprint Registry style.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`

### [P2] Numbered Scaffold Eyebrows
- **Why it matters**: The "What We Have Learned" section uses "01 / 02 / 03 / 04 / 05 / 06" display markers as visual layout fillers rather than representing a sequential workflow.
- **Fix**: Rework the markers or group the observations into thematic blocks rather than relying on sequential numbers.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable quieter`
