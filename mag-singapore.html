About MAG

MAG — Residential & Light Commercial Sliding Gate Specialist

MAG is a gate automation brand that has been distributed in Singapore for over 15 years, building a substantial installed base in the residential sliding gate market. MAG motors use a rack-and-pinion drive system — a motor-driven gear running along a toothed rack attached to the gate leaf — which provides more consistent torque across the full gate travel compared to friction-drive chain systems. The brand's product range covers sliding gate motors from 300kg to 1,000kg capacity, with models suitable for residential terrace homes through to light commercial premises.

MAG's strength in Singapore is its local distribution and service network. Spare parts — control boards, capacitors, drive motors, and limit switches — are stocked locally, and MAG-trained technicians are available through the distributor network for both installation and warranty service. For Singapore homeowners in the terrace and semi-detached segment, MAG provides a practical, well-supported sliding gate solution at a price point below European alternatives.

Securevision installs MAG sliding gate motors only. For swing gate automation, we specify FAAC. For boom barriers and road blockers, we specify FAAC.

Why We Carry MAG

Durability Built for Singapore

MAG offers the ideal balance of mechanical longevity and support speed for Singapore's intensive residential residential sliding gate usage.

⚙️

Rack-and-Pinion Drive Outlasts Friction Chain Systems

MAG's rack-and-pinion drive mechanism engages positively with the gate rack at every point. In Singapore's residential market, where chain-drive gate motors require annual tensioning, rack-and-pinion systems routinely operate maintenance-free for 5+ years.

🛠️

Local Spare Parts Availability Reduces Repair Downtime

MAG spare parts are stocked in Singapore, with same-day or next-day availability for common components. For homeowners, the difference between a same-day repair and a 3-week wait for imported parts is often the primary selection criterion.

💰

Competitive Price Point for Residential Segment

MAG's residential motor range delivers reliable rack-and-pinion automation at a price point accessible to Singapore's terrace house market. Consistently delivers acceptable total cost of ownership over a 7–10 year service life.

🔗

SECURE™ Integration

MAG sliding gate motors connect via dry contact relay to our access control and LPR systems — an authorised card swipe or licence plate recognition triggers the gate open command, with the access event logged to the VESTA dashboard alongside a timestamped CCTV clip of the vehicle entry.
