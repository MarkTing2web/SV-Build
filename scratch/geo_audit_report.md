## Implementation Plan
### Step 1: Inject JSON-LD Global Schema
- Create a standard `LocalBusiness` JSON-LD snippet targeting Singapore and inject it into the global header or footer include.
- Add `FAQPage` schema dynamically to pages with Q&A sections.
### Step 2: Standardize Missing Content Blocks
- **Audience Profiling:** Add a "Who This is For / Not For" component block to `solutions/*` and `portfolio/*` templates.
- **Competitive Alternatives:** Add "Why Choose Us over Alternatives" section in core service pages.
- **Nuance/Myths & Prerequisites:** Create reusable `<section>` blocks for common objections and preparation checklists and inject into the `templates/_template-solution-standard.html` and `templates/_template-portfolio-*.html`.
### Step 3: Enhance Risk Reversal & Cost Transparency
- Explicitly mention SLAs, support response times, and general pricing tiers/estimates on solution and systems pages to improve AI extraction.
